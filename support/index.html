Help

Support

Found a bug, a recipe that seems off, or have an idea? Open an issue and we'll take a look.

Restoring your purchase

If you bought the unlock and reinstalled or switched phones, open Dranks, scroll to the bottom of Home and tap Restore purchase while signed in to the same Apple Account.

Syncing between devices

Sign in to iCloud with the same Apple Account on each device and make sure iCloud is allowed for Dranks in Settings. Changes can take a minute to appear.

Sharing drinks

Share sends the drink's card and a link. With Dranks installed, the link opens the drink in the app, free. Without it, the link opens the drink's page on this site. A drink you made and shared while offline opens only in Dranks.

Deleting your data

Delete your rating from a drink's ratings in the app: tap Delete my rating. Deleting a drink you made also deletes the copies you shared. Your bar, lists and drinks live in your iCloud; removing the app and its iCloud data (Settings → your name → iCloud → Manage Storage) deletes them.
