Monkey Wrench

Light rum and fresh grapefruit juice over ice with a dash of bitters. The rum's answer to a Greyhound: dry, tart and about as easy as a drink gets.

Ingredients

  • 1½ oz white rum
  • 3 oz fresh grapefruit juice
  • 1 dash Angostura bitters

Steps

  1. Add ice cubes to a highball glass.
  2. Add 1½ oz white rum to the highball glass.
  3. Add 3 oz fresh grapefruit juice to the highball glass.
  4. Add 1 dash Angostura bitters to the highball glass.
  5. Give it a gentle stir.
