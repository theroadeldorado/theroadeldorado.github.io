Amaretto Mist

Amaretto poured over crushed ice with a squeeze of lime. The cold and the citrus keep the almond sweetness in check.

Ingredients

  • 2 oz amaretto
  • ¼ oz fresh lime juice

Steps

  1. Add crushed ice to a rocks glass.
  2. Add 2 oz amaretto to the rocks glass.
  3. Add ¼ oz fresh lime juice to the rocks glass.
  4. Give it a gentle stir.
  5. Garnish with a lime wedge on the rim.
