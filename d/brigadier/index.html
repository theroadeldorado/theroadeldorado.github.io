BUILT · IRISH COFFEE

Brigadier

Hot chocolate spiked with green Chartreuse and cherry liqueur. An après-ski mug: herbal, fruity and deeply warming.

Ingredients

  • 4 oz hot chocolate
  • 1 oz Green Chartreuse
  • 1 oz cherry liqueur

Steps

  1. Add 4 oz hot chocolate to an Irish coffee glass.
  2. Add 1 oz Green Chartreuse to the Irish coffee glass.
  3. Add 1 oz cherry liqueur to the Irish coffee glass.
  4. Give it a gentle stir.
