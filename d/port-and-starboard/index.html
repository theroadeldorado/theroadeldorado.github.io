Port and Starboard

Pomegranate grenadine with crème de menthe layered on top. Named for the two running lights on either side of a ship: sweet fruit underneath, cool mint above.

Ingredients

  • ½ oz pomegranate grenadine
  • ½ oz green crème de menthe, layered

Steps

  1. Chill a shot glass, the grenadine and the crème de menthe first: cold layers settle more cleanly.
  2. Add ½ oz pomegranate grenadine to the shot glass.
  3. Hold a bar spoon upside down just above the grenadine in the shot glass and slowly pour the crème de menthe over its back so it settles on top.
