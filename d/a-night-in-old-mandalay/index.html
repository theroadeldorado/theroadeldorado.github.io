SHAKEN · HIGHBALL

A Night in Old Mandalay

Light and aged rum shaken with fresh orange and lemon, then lengthened with ginger ale. A gently spiced, citrusy long drink.

Ingredients

  • 1 oz white rum
  • 1 oz aged rum
  • 1 oz fresh orange juice
  • ½ oz fresh lemon juice
  • 3 oz ginger ale

Steps

  1. Fill the shaker with ice. Add ice cubes to a highball glass.
  2. Add 1 oz white rum to the shaker.
  3. Add 1 oz aged rum to the shaker.
  4. Add 1 oz fresh orange juice to the shaker.
  5. Add ½ oz fresh lemon juice to the shaker.
  6. Cap the shaker and shake hard for about 12 seconds.
  7. Strain into the highball glass.
  8. Top with 3 oz ginger ale.
  9. Express a lemon peel over the drink and drop it in.
