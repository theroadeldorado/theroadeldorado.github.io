Dark 'n' Stormy

Ginger beer with dark rum floated on top.

Ingredients

  • 4 oz ginger beer
  • 2 oz dark rum

Steps

  1. Add ice cubes to a highball glass.
  2. Add 4 oz ginger beer to the highball glass.
  3. Over the back of a spoon, gently pour 2 oz dark rum onto the drink so it floats on top.
  4. Garnish with a lime wedge on the rim.
