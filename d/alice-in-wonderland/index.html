BUILT · ROCKS

Alice in Wonderland

Amaretto, Grand Marnier and Southern Comfort in equal parts over ice. Sweet, nutty and orange-scented: a curious little after-dinner sipper.

Ingredients

  • 1 oz amaretto
  • 1 oz Grand Marnier
  • 1 oz Southern Comfort

Steps

  1. Add ice cubes to a rocks glass.
  2. Add 1 oz amaretto to the rocks glass.
  3. Add 1 oz Grand Marnier to the rocks glass.
  4. Add 1 oz Southern Comfort to the rocks glass.
  5. Give it a gentle stir.
