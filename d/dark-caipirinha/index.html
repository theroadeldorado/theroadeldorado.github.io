Dark Caipirinha

The Caipirinha sweetened with demerara instead of plain sugar: lime wedges crushed in the glass, cachaça and crushed ice. Deeper, with a toffee edge.

Ingredients

  • ½ lime, cut into wedges
  • ½ oz demerara syrup
  • 2 oz cachaça

Steps

  1. Muddle ½ lime (cut into wedges) in a rocks glass.
  2. Add crushed ice to the rocks glass.
  3. Add ½ oz demerara syrup to the rocks glass.
  4. Add 2 oz cachaça to the rocks glass.
  5. Give it a gentle stir.
  6. Garnish with a lime wheel on the rim.
