SHAKEN · COUPE

Bobby Burns

Scotch and sweet vermouth in equal parts with a touch of Bénédictine. Harry Craddock called it one of the very best whisky cocktails; it's a honeyed, herbal Rob Roy for Burns Night.

Ingredients

  • 1½ oz blended Scotch
  • 1½ oz sweet vermouth
  • ¼ oz Bénédictine

Steps

  1. Fill the shaker with ice.
  2. Add 1½ oz blended Scotch to the shaker.
  3. Add 1½ oz sweet vermouth to the shaker.
  4. Add ¼ oz Bénédictine to the shaker.
  5. Cap the shaker and shake hard for about 12 seconds.
  6. Strain into a chilled coupe.
  7. Express a lemon peel over the drink and drop it in.
