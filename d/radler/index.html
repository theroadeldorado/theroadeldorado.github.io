BUILT · HIGHBALL

Radler

Cold lager cut with fresh, fizzy homemade lemonade over ice. The Bavarian cyclist's thirst-quencher: half beer, half lemonade, all refreshment.

Ingredients

  • 1 oz fresh lemon juice
  • 1 oz simple syrup
  • 1½ oz soda water
  • 3 oz lager

Steps

  1. Add ice cubes to a highball glass.
  2. Add 1 oz fresh lemon juice to the highball glass.
  3. Add 1 oz simple syrup to the highball glass.
  4. Add 1½ oz soda water to the highball glass.
  5. Top with 3 oz lager.
  6. Give it a gentle stir.
  7. Garnish with a lemon wheel on the rim.
