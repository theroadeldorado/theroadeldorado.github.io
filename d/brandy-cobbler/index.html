Brandy Cobbler

Brandy over a glass packed with crushed ice, barely sweetened and freshened with soda. Old Mr. Boston's cobbler: cold, light and dressed with fruit.

Ingredients

  • 2 oz cognac
  • ¼ oz simple syrup
  • 2 oz soda water

Steps

  1. Add crushed ice to a highball glass.
  2. Add 2 oz cognac to the highball glass.
  3. Add ¼ oz simple syrup to the highball glass.
  4. Top with 2 oz soda water.
  5. Give it a gentle stir.
  6. Garnish with an orange slice. Add a straw.
