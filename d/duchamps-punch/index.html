SHAKEN · ROCKS

Duchamp's Punch

Pisco shaken with lime, pineapple, sugar and elderflower liqueur, plus a couple of dashes of bitters, over ice. Floral, tropical and tart.

Ingredients

  • 2 oz pisco
  • ¾ oz fresh lime juice
  • ¾ oz pineapple juice
  • ¼ oz simple syrup
  • ½ oz elderflower liqueur
  • 2 dashes Angostura bitters

Steps

  1. Fill the shaker with ice. Add ice cubes to a rocks glass.
  2. Add 2 oz pisco to the shaker.
  3. Add ¾ oz fresh lime juice to the shaker.
  4. Add ¾ oz pineapple juice to the shaker.
  5. Add ¼ oz simple syrup to the shaker.
  6. Add ½ oz elderflower liqueur to the shaker.
  7. Add 2 dashes Angostura bitters to the shaker.
  8. Cap the shaker and shake hard for about 12 seconds.
  9. Strain into the rocks glass.
