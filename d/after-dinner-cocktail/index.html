SHAKEN · COUPE

After Dinner Cocktail

Apricot liqueur and triple sec in equal parts, sharpened with the juice of a lime. An Old Mr. Boston nightcap, sweet-tart and bright.

Ingredients

  • 1 oz apricot brandy
  • 1 oz Cointreau
  • ¾ oz fresh lime juice

Steps

  1. Fill the shaker with ice.
  2. Add 1 oz apricot brandy to the shaker.
  3. Add 1 oz Cointreau to the shaker.
  4. Add ¾ oz fresh lime juice to the shaker.
  5. Cap the shaker and shake hard for about 12 seconds.
  6. Strain into a chilled coupe.
  7. Garnish with a lime wheel on the rim.
