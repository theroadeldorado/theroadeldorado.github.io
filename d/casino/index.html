SHAKEN · COUPE

Casino

Gin, maraschino and lemon with orange bitters: an Aviation without the crème de violette.

Ingredients

  • 1¼ oz gin
  • ¼ oz maraschino liqueur
  • ¼ oz fresh lemon juice
  • 2 dashes orange bitters

Steps

  1. Fill the shaker with ice.
  2. Add 1¼ oz gin to the shaker.
  3. Add ¼ oz maraschino liqueur to the shaker.
  4. Add ¼ oz fresh lemon juice to the shaker.
  5. Add 2 dashes orange bitters to the shaker.
  6. Cap the shaker and shake hard for about 12 seconds.
  7. Strain into a chilled coupe.
  8. Garnish with a cherry and a lemon peel.
