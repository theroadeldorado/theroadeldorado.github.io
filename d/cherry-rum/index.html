SHAKEN · COUPE

Cherry Rum

Light rum with a little cherry brandy and a spoonful of cream, shaken and served up, as Old Mr. Boston poured it. Small, smooth and softly fruity.

Ingredients

  • 1¼ oz white rum
  • ¼ oz cherry liqueur
  • ½ oz heavy cream

Steps

  1. Fill the shaker with ice.
  2. Add 1¼ oz white rum to the shaker.
  3. Add ¼ oz cherry liqueur to the shaker.
  4. Add ½ oz heavy cream to the shaker.
  5. Cap the shaker and shake hard for about 12 seconds.
  6. Strain into a chilled coupe.
