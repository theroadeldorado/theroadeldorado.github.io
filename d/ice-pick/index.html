BUILT · HIGHBALL

Ice Pick

Vodka over ice with chilled black tea, fresh lemon and a little sugar. Spiked lemon iced tea for a hot afternoon: brisk, lightly sweet and dangerously easy.

Ingredients

  • 1½ oz vodka
  • 4 oz black tea, brewed and chilled
  • ½ oz fresh lemon juice
  • ½ oz simple syrup

Steps

  1. Add ice cubes to a highball glass.
  2. Add 1½ oz vodka to the highball glass.
  3. Add 4 oz black tea (brewed and chilled) to the highball glass.
  4. Add ½ oz fresh lemon juice to the highball glass.
  5. Add ½ oz simple syrup to the highball glass.
  6. Give it a gentle stir.
  7. Garnish with a lemon wheel on the rim.
