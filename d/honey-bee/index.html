SHAKEN · COUPE

Honey Bee

Aged rum shaken with fresh lemon and honey: the Bee's Knees with rum where the gin used to be. Rounder and richer than the original, with the honey taming the rum's funk.

Ingredients

  • 2 oz aged rum
  • ¾ oz fresh lemon juice
  • ¾ oz honey syrup

Steps

  1. Fill the shaker with ice.
  2. Add 2 oz aged rum to the shaker.
  3. Add ¾ oz fresh lemon juice to the shaker.
  4. Add ¾ oz honey syrup to the shaker.
  5. Cap the shaker and shake hard for about 12 seconds.
  6. Strain into a chilled coupe.
  7. Express a lemon peel over the drink and drop it in.
