Gin Smash

Mint pressed with a little sugar, then gin and a glassful of shaved ice. Jerry Thomas's pocket-sized Julep, cool and minty, made with gin.

Ingredients

  • 8 mint leaves
  • ¼ oz simple syrup
  • 2 oz gin

Steps

  1. Muddle 8 mint leaves in a rocks glass.
  2. Add crushed ice to the rocks glass.
  3. Add ¼ oz simple syrup to the rocks glass.
  4. Add 2 oz gin to the rocks glass.
  5. Give it a gentle stir.
  6. Garnish with a sprig of mint. Add a straw.
