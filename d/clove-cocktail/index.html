Clove Cocktail

Sweet vermouth stirred with sloe gin and a sweet wine. Mr. Boston's mellow aperitif tastes of plum, raisin and warm spice, with barely any bite.

Ingredients

  • 1 oz sweet vermouth
  • ½ oz sloe gin
  • ½ oz cream sherry, or Muscatel, or any sweet dessert wine

Steps

  1. Fill the mixing glass with ice.
  2. Add 1 oz sweet vermouth to the mixing glass.
  3. Add ½ oz sloe gin to the mixing glass.
  4. Add ½ oz cream sherry (or Muscatel, or any sweet dessert wine) to the mixing glass.
  5. Stir with a bar spoon for about 30 seconds, until well chilled.
  6. Strain into a chilled coupe.
