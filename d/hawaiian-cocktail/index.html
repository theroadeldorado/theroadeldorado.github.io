SHAKEN · COUPE

Hawaiian Cocktail

Gin shaken with a splash of pineapple and a little orange liqueur, then served up. A dry, gin-forward cocktail from Old Mr. Boston with only a hint of the islands.

Ingredients

  • 2 oz gin
  • ½ oz pineapple juice
  • ½ oz Cointreau

Steps

  1. Fill the shaker with ice.
  2. Add 2 oz gin to the shaker.
  3. Add ½ oz pineapple juice to the shaker.
  4. Add ½ oz Cointreau to the shaker.
  5. Cap the shaker and shake hard for about 12 seconds.
  6. Strain into a chilled coupe.
  7. Garnish with a pineapple wedge on the rim.
