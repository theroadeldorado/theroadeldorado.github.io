SHAKEN · HIGHBALL

Space Odyssey

Overproof rum and coconut rum shaken with pineapple, fresh orange and a touch of grenadine. Sunny and fruity on the surface, with a launch-pad kick underneath.

Ingredients

  • 1 oz overproof rum
  • 1 oz coconut rum
  • 1 oz pineapple juice
  • 2½ oz fresh orange juice
  • ¼ oz pomegranate grenadine

Steps

  1. Fill the shaker with ice. Add ice cubes to a highball glass.
  2. Add 1 oz overproof rum to the shaker.
  3. Add 1 oz coconut rum to the shaker.
  4. Add 1 oz pineapple juice to the shaker.
  5. Add 2½ oz fresh orange juice to the shaker.
  6. Add ¼ oz pomegranate grenadine to the shaker.
  7. Cap the shaker and shake hard for about 12 seconds.
  8. Strain into the highball glass.
  9. Drop in a cocktail cherry.
