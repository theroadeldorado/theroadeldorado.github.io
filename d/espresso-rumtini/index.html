Espresso Rumtini

An Espresso Martini built on rum, with coffee liqueur and a touch of vanilla, shaken hard over fresh espresso until it foams.

Ingredients

  • 1½ oz white rum
  • ¾ oz coffee liqueur
  • 1 oz espresso
  • ¼ oz vanilla syrup

Steps

  1. Fill the shaker with ice.
  2. Add 1½ oz white rum to the shaker.
  3. Add ¾ oz coffee liqueur to the shaker.
  4. Add 1 oz espresso to the shaker.
  5. Add ¼ oz vanilla syrup to the shaker.
  6. Cap the shaker and shake hard for about 12 seconds.
  7. Strain into a chilled martini glass.
  8. Float three coffee beans on top.
