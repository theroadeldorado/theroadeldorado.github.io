Blue Lagoon

Vodka and blue curaçao with fresh citrus and sugar, lengthened with soda. Credited to Andy MacElhone of Harry's New York Bar in Paris: bright, zesty and easy to drink.

Ingredients

  • 1¼ oz vodka
  • 1 oz blue curaçao
  • ¾ oz fresh lime juice
  • ¼ oz fresh lemon juice
  • ½ oz simple syrup
  • Soda water, to top

Steps

  1. Add ice cubes to a highball glass.
  2. Add 1¼ oz vodka to the highball glass.
  3. Add 1 oz blue curaçao to the highball glass.
  4. Add ¾ oz fresh lime juice to the highball glass.
  5. Add ¼ oz fresh lemon juice to the highball glass.
  6. Add ½ oz simple syrup to the highball glass.
  7. Top with soda water.
  8. Give it a gentle stir.
  9. Garnish with a cherry and an orange wheel.
