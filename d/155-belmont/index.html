BLENDED · WINE

155 Belmont

Two rums and vodka blended with fresh orange juice into a strong, frosty slush. It goes down like a breakfast smoothie, so pace yourself.

Ingredients

  • 1½ oz white rum
  • ¾ oz dark rum
  • ¾ oz vodka
  • 1½ oz fresh orange juice

Steps

  1. Blend 1½ oz white rum, ¾ oz dark rum, ¾ oz vodka and 1½ oz fresh orange juice with ½ cup of ice until smooth, then pour into a wine glass.
  2. Add a straw.
