Abilene

Dark rum with peach purée and fresh orange juice over ice. Soft, sunny and a good brunch drink.

Ingredients

  • 1½ oz dark rum
  • 1½ oz peach purée
  • 3 oz fresh orange juice

Steps

  1. Add ice cubes to a highball glass.
  2. Add 1½ oz dark rum to the highball glass.
  3. Add 1½ oz peach purée to the highball glass.
  4. Add 3 oz fresh orange juice to the highball glass.
  5. Give it a gentle stir.
  6. Garnish with an orange wheel on the rim.
