STIRRED · ROCKS

Sazerac

New Orleans in a glass: rye, sugar and Peychaud's in an absinthe-rinsed glass.

Ingredients

  • 2 oz rye whiskey
  • ¼ oz simple syrup
  • 3 dashes Peychaud's bitters
  • 1 barspoon absinthe, to rinse the glass

Steps

  1. Rinse a rocks glass with 1 barspoon of absinthe: swirl to coat, then pour out the excess.
  2. Fill the mixing glass with ice.
  3. Add ¼ oz simple syrup to the mixing glass.
  4. Add 3 dashes Peychaud's bitters to the mixing glass.
  5. Add 2 oz rye whiskey to the mixing glass.
  6. Stir with a bar spoon for about 30 seconds, until well chilled.
  7. Strain into the rocks glass.
  8. Express a lemon peel over the drink, then discard it.
