STIRRED · COUPE

London Town

Gin stirred cold with maraschino and two dashes of orange bitters. Crisp juniper with a faint cherry-pit sweetness and a little citrus perfume on the finish.

Ingredients

  • 1½ oz gin
  • ½ oz maraschino liqueur
  • 2 dashes orange bitters

Steps

  1. Fill the mixing glass with ice.
  2. Add 1½ oz gin to the mixing glass.
  3. Add ½ oz maraschino liqueur to the mixing glass.
  4. Add 2 dashes orange bitters to the mixing glass.
  5. Stir with a bar spoon for about 30 seconds, until well chilled.
  6. Strain into a chilled coupe.
