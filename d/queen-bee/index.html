STIRRED · COUPE

Queen Bee

Coffee liqueur, citrus vodka and cream sherry stirred cold and served up. A small, sippable nightcap: roasted coffee, a lift of lemon peel and a nutty, raisin-soft finish.

Ingredients

  • 1 oz coffee liqueur
  • 1½ oz citrus vodka
  • ½ oz cream sherry

Steps

  1. Fill the mixing glass with ice.
  2. Add 1 oz coffee liqueur to the mixing glass.
  3. Add 1½ oz citrus vodka to the mixing glass.
  4. Add ½ oz cream sherry to the mixing glass.
  5. Stir with a bar spoon for about 30 seconds, until well chilled.
  6. Strain into a chilled coupe.
