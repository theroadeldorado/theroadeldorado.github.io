SHAKEN · HIGHBALL

A Day at the Beach

Coconut rum, amaretto and fresh orange juice, with grenadine slipped in to settle at the bottom. Sweet, nutty and tropical.

Ingredients

  • 1½ oz coconut rum
  • ½ oz amaretto
  • 3½ oz fresh orange juice
  • ½ oz pomegranate grenadine, poured last so it sinks

Steps

  1. Fill the shaker with ice. Add ice cubes to a highball glass.
  2. Add 1½ oz coconut rum to the shaker.
  3. Add ½ oz amaretto to the shaker.
  4. Add 3½ oz fresh orange juice to the shaker.
  5. Cap the shaker and shake hard for about 12 seconds.
  6. Strain into the highball glass.
  7. Slowly pour ½ oz pomegranate grenadine down the inside of the highball glass so it settles at the bottom.
  8. Garnish with an orange wheel on the rim. Add a straw.
