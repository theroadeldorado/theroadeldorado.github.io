Passion Fruit Martini

Vodka shaken hard with passion fruit syrup and fresh lime, then served up. Tropical, perfumed and tart: a simple passion fruit sour in a cocktail glass.

Ingredients

  • 2 oz vodka
  • ¾ oz passion fruit syrup
  • ¾ oz fresh lime juice

Steps

  1. Fill the shaker with ice.
  2. Add 2 oz vodka to the shaker.
  3. Add ¾ oz passion fruit syrup to the shaker.
  4. Add ¾ oz fresh lime juice to the shaker.
  5. Cap the shaker and shake hard for about 12 seconds.
  6. Strain into a chilled martini glass.
