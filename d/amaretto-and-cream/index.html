Amaretto and Cream

Amaretto shaken with cream until smooth, with nutmeg on top. Two ingredients, and it tastes like almond ice cream.

Ingredients

  • 1½ oz amaretto
  • 1 oz heavy cream

Steps

  1. Fill the shaker with ice.
  2. Add 1½ oz amaretto to the shaker.
  3. Add 1 oz heavy cream to the shaker.
  4. Cap the shaker and shake hard for about 12 seconds.
  5. Strain into a chilled coupe.
  6. Grate a little nutmeg over the top.
