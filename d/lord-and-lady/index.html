BUILT · ROCKS

Lord and Lady

Dark rum with a little coffee liqueur over one big cube. Molasses and roast coffee in a short, slow after-dinner sipper; think of it as a Black Russian that sailed to Jamaica.

Ingredients

  • 1½ oz dark rum
  • ½ oz coffee liqueur

Steps

  1. Add a big ice cube to a rocks glass.
  2. Add 1½ oz dark rum to the rocks glass.
  3. Add ½ oz coffee liqueur to the rocks glass.
  4. Give it a gentle stir.
