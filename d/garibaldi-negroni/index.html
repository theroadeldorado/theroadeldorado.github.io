Garibaldi Negroni

Gin and Campari lengthened with fresh orange juice over ice. Half Negroni, half Garibaldi: bittersweet and juicy, made for an early aperitivo.

Ingredients

  • 1 oz gin
  • 1 oz Campari
  • 3 oz fresh orange juice

Steps

  1. Add ice cubes to a highball glass.
  2. Add 1 oz gin to the highball glass.
  3. Add 1 oz Campari to the highball glass.
  4. Add 3 oz fresh orange juice to the highball glass.
  5. Give it a gentle stir.
  6. Express an orange peel over the drink and drop it in.
