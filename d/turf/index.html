STIRRED · COUPE

Turf

Gin and dry vermouth in equal parts, stirred with a whisper of absinthe and finished with orange peel. A bone-dry Martini from the old mixing manuals, with a cool thread of anise in the background.

Ingredients

  • 1½ oz gin
  • 1½ oz dry vermouth
  • ½ barspoon absinthe

Steps

  1. Fill the mixing glass with ice.
  2. Add 1½ oz gin to the mixing glass.
  3. Add 1½ oz dry vermouth to the mixing glass.
  4. Add ½ barspoon absinthe to the mixing glass.
  5. Stir with a bar spoon for about 30 seconds, until well chilled.
  6. Strain into a chilled coupe.
  7. Express an orange peel over the drink and drop it in.
