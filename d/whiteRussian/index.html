White Russian

Vodka and coffee liqueur with a pour of cream.

Ingredients

  • 2 oz vodka
  • 1 oz coffee liqueur
  • 1 oz heavy cream

Steps

  1. Add ice cubes to a rocks glass.
  2. Add 2 oz vodka to the rocks glass.
  3. Add 1 oz coffee liqueur to the rocks glass.
  4. Add 1 oz heavy cream to the rocks glass.
  5. Give it a gentle stir.
  6. Grate a little nutmeg over the top. Add a stirrer.
