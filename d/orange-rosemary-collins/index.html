Orange Rosemary Collins

A rosemary sprig bruised with sugar, shaken with gin, fresh orange and lemon, then topped with soda over ice. Piney and bright: a wintry Collins that smells like a herb garden.

Ingredients

  • 1 rosemary sprig
  • ¾ oz simple syrup
  • 2 oz gin
  • 1 oz fresh orange juice
  • ¾ oz fresh lemon juice
  • Soda water, to top

Steps

  1. Muddle 1 rosemary sprig in the shaker.
  2. Fill the shaker with ice. Add ice cubes to a highball glass.
  3. Add ¾ oz simple syrup to the shaker.
  4. Add 2 oz gin to the shaker.
  5. Add 1 oz fresh orange juice to the shaker.
  6. Add ¾ oz fresh lemon juice to the shaker.
  7. Cap the shaker and shake hard for about 12 seconds.
  8. Strain into the highball glass.
  9. Top with soda water.
  10. Garnish with a sprig of rosemary.
