Atomic Lokade

Vodka and two orange liqueurs with fresh lemon and sugar, topped with soda: a sparkling spiked lemonade. Bright, tart and made for a crowd.

Ingredients

  • 1 oz vodka
  • ½ oz blue curaçao
  • ½ oz Cointreau
  • ¾ oz fresh lemon juice
  • ½ oz simple syrup
  • Soda water, to top

Steps

  1. Add ice cubes to a highball glass.
  2. Add 1 oz vodka to the highball glass.
  3. Add ½ oz blue curaçao to the highball glass.
  4. Add ½ oz Cointreau to the highball glass.
  5. Add ¾ oz fresh lemon juice to the highball glass.
  6. Add ½ oz simple syrup to the highball glass.
  7. Top with soda water.
  8. Give it a gentle stir.
  9. Garnish with a lemon wheel on the rim.
