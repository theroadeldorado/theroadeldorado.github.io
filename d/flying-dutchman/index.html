Flying Dutchman

Gin shaken with just a splash of orange liqueur and served over ice, as Old Mr. Boston listed it. Crisp and nearly all gin, with an orange-peel finish.

Ingredients

  • 2 oz gin
  • ¼ oz Cointreau

Steps

  1. Fill the shaker with ice. Add a big ice cube to a rocks glass.
  2. Add 2 oz gin to the shaker.
  3. Add ¼ oz Cointreau to the shaker.
  4. Cap the shaker and shake hard for about 12 seconds.
  5. Strain into the rocks glass.
  6. Express an orange peel over the drink and drop it in.
