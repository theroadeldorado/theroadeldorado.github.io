STIRRED · COUPE

Blackthorn

Sloe gin and sweet vermouth, two to one, with a dash of orange bitters. Named for the hedgerow shrub whose sloes flavor the gin; tart, plummy and soft.

Ingredients

  • 2 oz sloe gin
  • 1 oz sweet vermouth
  • 1 dash orange bitters

Steps

  1. Fill the mixing glass with ice.
  2. Add 2 oz sloe gin to the mixing glass.
  3. Add 1 oz sweet vermouth to the mixing glass.
  4. Add 1 dash orange bitters to the mixing glass.
  5. Stir with a bar spoon for about 30 seconds, until well chilled.
  6. Strain into a chilled coupe.
  7. Express a lemon peel over the drink and drop it in.
