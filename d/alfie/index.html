SHAKEN · COUPE

Alfie

Lemon vodka shaken with a splash of pineapple and a little orange liqueur. Crisp and citrusy, with just a hint of the tropics.

Ingredients

  • 2 oz citrus vodka
  • ¼ oz Cointreau
  • ½ oz pineapple juice

Steps

  1. Fill the shaker with ice.
  2. Add 2 oz citrus vodka to the shaker.
  3. Add ¼ oz Cointreau to the shaker.
  4. Add ½ oz pineapple juice to the shaker.
  5. Cap the shaker and shake hard for about 12 seconds.
  6. Strain into a chilled coupe.
  7. Express a lemon peel over the drink and drop it in.
