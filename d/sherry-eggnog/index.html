Sherry Eggnog

Sweet sherry, a whole egg, sugar and milk shaken until frothy and dusted with nutmeg. A light, nutty single-serve eggnog that's low on alcohol and big on comfort.

Ingredients

  • 2 oz cream sherry
  • 1½ oz beaten egg
  • ¼ oz simple syrup
  • 4 oz milk

Steps

  1. Fill the shaker with ice.
  2. Add 2 oz cream sherry to the shaker.
  3. Add 1½ oz beaten egg to the shaker.
  4. Add ¼ oz simple syrup to the shaker.
  5. Add 4 oz milk to the shaker.
  6. Cap the shaker and shake hard for about 12 seconds.
  7. Strain into a chilled highball glass.
  8. Grate a little nutmeg over the top.
