STIRRED · ROCKS

Boulevardier

The Negroni's warmer cousin, with bourbon in place of gin.

Ingredients

  • 1½ oz bourbon
  • 1 oz Campari
  • 1 oz sweet vermouth

Steps

  1. Fill the mixing glass with ice. Add an ice sphere to a rocks glass.
  2. Add 1½ oz bourbon to the mixing glass.
  3. Add 1 oz Campari to the mixing glass.
  4. Add 1 oz sweet vermouth to the mixing glass.
  5. Stir with a bar spoon for about 30 seconds, until well chilled.
  6. Strain into the rocks glass.
  7. Express an orange peel over the drink and drop it in.
