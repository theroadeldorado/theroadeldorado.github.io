SHAKEN · COUPE

Valencia

Apricot brandy shaken with fresh orange juice and four dashes of orange bitters, as the Savoy has it. Soft stone fruit and sunny orange, sweet but with a bitter edge from the dashes.

Ingredients

  • 4 dashes orange bitters
  • 1 oz fresh orange juice
  • 2 oz apricot brandy

Steps

  1. Fill the shaker with ice.
  2. Add 4 dashes orange bitters to the shaker.
  3. Add 1 oz fresh orange juice to the shaker.
  4. Add 2 oz apricot brandy to the shaker.
  5. Cap the shaker and shake hard for about 12 seconds.
  6. Strain into a chilled coupe.
  7. Express an orange peel over the drink and drop it in.
