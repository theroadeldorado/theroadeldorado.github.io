Rum Sour

Light rum shaken with fresh lemon and sugar and served up. A Rum Sour from the Embury era, balanced the modern way: like a Daiquiri, but the lemon makes it rounder and a little less sharp.

Ingredients

  • 2 oz white rum
  • ¾ oz fresh lemon juice
  • ¾ oz simple syrup

Steps

  1. Fill the shaker with ice.
  2. Add 2 oz white rum to the shaker.
  3. Add ¾ oz fresh lemon juice to the shaker.
  4. Add ¾ oz simple syrup to the shaker.
  5. Cap the shaker and shake hard for about 12 seconds.
  6. Strain into a chilled coupe.
  7. Garnish with an orange slice.
