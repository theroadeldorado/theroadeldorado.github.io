Orange Push-up

Spiced rum shaken with fresh orange juice, real grenadine and a squeeze of lemon. Named for the frozen orange treat on a stick, it tastes like a creamsicle's boozy cousin.

Ingredients

  • 1½ oz spiced rum
  • ½ oz pomegranate grenadine
  • 4 oz fresh orange juice
  • ½ oz fresh lemon juice
  • ¼ oz simple syrup

Steps

  1. Fill the shaker with ice. Add ice cubes to a hurricane glass.
  2. Add 1½ oz spiced rum to the shaker.
  3. Add ½ oz pomegranate grenadine to the shaker.
  4. Add 4 oz fresh orange juice to the shaker.
  5. Add ½ oz fresh lemon juice to the shaker.
  6. Add ¼ oz simple syrup to the shaker.
  7. Cap the shaker and shake hard for about 12 seconds.
  8. Strain into the hurricane glass.
  9. Garnish with an orange wheel on the rim.
