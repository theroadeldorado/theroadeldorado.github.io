SHAKEN · COUPE

Gagliardo

Vodka and peach schnapps shaken with lemon, a splash of Galliano and rose syrup. A fragrant sour: stone fruit up front, vanilla and anise behind, and a perfumed finish.

Ingredients

  • 1¼ oz vodka
  • ½ oz peach schnapps
  • ¾ oz fresh lemon juice
  • ¼ oz Galliano
  • ¼ oz rose syrup

Steps

  1. Fill the shaker with ice.
  2. Add 1¼ oz vodka to the shaker.
  3. Add ½ oz peach schnapps to the shaker.
  4. Add ¾ oz fresh lemon juice to the shaker.
  5. Add ¼ oz Galliano to the shaker.
  6. Add ¼ oz rose syrup to the shaker.
  7. Cap the shaker and shake hard for about 12 seconds.
  8. Strain into a chilled coupe.
  9. Express a lemon peel over the drink and drop it in.
