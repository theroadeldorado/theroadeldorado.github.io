Aperol Spritz

Prosecco, Aperol and a splash of soda. Sunny and low-proof.

Ingredients

  • 3 oz Prosecco
  • 2 oz Aperol
  • 1 oz soda water

Steps

  1. Add ice cubes to a wine glass.
  2. Add 3 oz Prosecco to the wine glass.
  3. Add 2 oz Aperol to the wine glass.
  4. Top with 1 oz soda water.
  5. Give it a gentle stir.
  6. Garnish with an orange slice.
