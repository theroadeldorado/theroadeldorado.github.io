Sloe Gin Cocktail

Sloe gin stirred with dry and sweet vermouth in the Savoy's proportions. Plummy, tart and lightly bitter, like a fruit-forward Perfect Martini.

Ingredients

  • ¾ oz dry vermouth
  • ¾ oz sweet vermouth
  • 1½ oz sloe gin

Steps

  1. Fill the mixing glass with ice.
  2. Add ¾ oz dry vermouth to the mixing glass.
  3. Add ¾ oz sweet vermouth to the mixing glass.
  4. Add 1½ oz sloe gin to the mixing glass.
  5. Stir with a bar spoon for about 30 seconds, until well chilled.
  6. Strain into a chilled coupe.
  7. Express a lemon peel over the drink and drop it in.
