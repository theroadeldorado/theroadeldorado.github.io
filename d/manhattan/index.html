STIRRED · COUPE

Manhattan

Rye and sweet vermouth with a couple of dashes of bitters. Rich, spicy and smooth.

Ingredients

  • 2 oz rye whiskey
  • 1 oz sweet vermouth
  • 2 dashes Angostura bitters

Steps

  1. Fill the mixing glass with ice.
  2. Add 2 oz rye whiskey to the mixing glass.
  3. Add 1 oz sweet vermouth to the mixing glass.
  4. Add 2 dashes Angostura bitters to the mixing glass.
  5. Stir with a bar spoon for about 30 seconds, until well chilled.
  6. Strain into a chilled coupe.
  7. Drop in a cocktail cherry.
