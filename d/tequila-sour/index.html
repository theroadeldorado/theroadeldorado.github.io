Tequila Sour

Blanco tequila, fresh lemon and sugar shaken hard and served up with a cherry. The plain sour formula with agave at its heart: tart and clean, with an earthy, peppery finish.

Ingredients

  • 2 oz blanco tequila
  • ¾ oz fresh lemon juice
  • ¾ oz simple syrup

Steps

  1. Fill the shaker with ice.
  2. Add 2 oz blanco tequila to the shaker.
  3. Add ¾ oz fresh lemon juice to the shaker.
  4. Add ¾ oz simple syrup to the shaker.
  5. Cap the shaker and shake hard for about 12 seconds.
  6. Strain into a chilled coupe.
  7. Drop in a cocktail cherry.
