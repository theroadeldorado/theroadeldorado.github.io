Sea Breeze

Vodka with cranberry and fresh grapefruit juice. Tart and easygoing.

Ingredients

  • 1¼ oz vodka
  • 4 oz cranberry juice
  • 1 oz fresh grapefruit juice

Steps

  1. Add ice cubes to a highball glass.
  2. Add 1¼ oz vodka to the highball glass.
  3. Add 4 oz cranberry juice to the highball glass.
  4. Add 1 oz fresh grapefruit juice to the highball glass.
  5. Give it a gentle stir.
  6. Garnish with a lime wedge on the rim. Add a straw.
