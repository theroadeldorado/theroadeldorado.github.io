SHAKEN · COUPE

Grasshopper

Crème de menthe, crème de cacao and cream: an after-dinner mint you can drink.

Ingredients

  • ¾ oz green crème de menthe
  • ¾ oz crème de cacao
  • ¾ oz heavy cream

Steps

  1. Fill the shaker with ice.
  2. Add ¾ oz green crème de menthe to the shaker.
  3. Add ¾ oz crème de cacao to the shaker.
  4. Add ¾ oz heavy cream to the shaker.
  5. Cap the shaker and shake hard for about 12 seconds.
  6. Strain into a chilled coupe.
  7. Garnish with a sprig of mint.
