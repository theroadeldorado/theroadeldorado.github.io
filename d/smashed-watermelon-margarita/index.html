Smashed Watermelon Margarita

Ripe watermelon and mint crushed in the shaker, then shaken with tequila, grapefruit, lime and a little agave and poured over crushed ice in a salted glass. Juicy, cooling and only lightly sweet.

Ingredients

  • 6 watermelon cubes
  • 6 mint leaves
  • 2 oz blanco tequila
  • 1 oz fresh grapefruit juice
  • ½ oz fresh lime juice
  • ¼ oz agave syrup

Steps

  1. Wet the rim of a highball glass and roll it in salt.
  2. Muddle 6 watermelon cubes in the shaker.
  3. Muddle 6 mint leaves in the shaker.
  4. Fill the shaker with ice. Add crushed ice to the highball glass.
  5. Add 2 oz blanco tequila to the shaker.
  6. Add 1 oz fresh grapefruit juice to the shaker.
  7. Add ½ oz fresh lime juice to the shaker.
  8. Add ¼ oz agave syrup to the shaker.
  9. Cap the shaker and shake hard for about 12 seconds.
  10. Strain into the highball glass.
  11. Garnish with a sprig of mint. Add a straw.
