SHAKEN · ROCKS

Vesuvio

Light rum and sweet vermouth shaken with lemon, sugar and egg white and poured over ice. A silky, gently tart rum sour, with the vermouth adding a little spice and depth under the foam.

Ingredients

  • 1½ oz white rum
  • ¾ oz sweet vermouth
  • ¾ oz fresh lemon juice
  • ½ oz simple syrup
  • ¾ oz egg white

Steps

  1. Fill the shaker with ice. Add ice cubes to a rocks glass.
  2. Add 1½ oz white rum to the shaker.
  3. Add ¾ oz sweet vermouth to the shaker.
  4. Add ¾ oz fresh lemon juice to the shaker.
  5. Add ½ oz simple syrup to the shaker.
  6. Add ¾ oz egg white to the shaker.
  7. Cap the shaker and shake hard for about 12 seconds.
  8. Strain into the rocks glass.
