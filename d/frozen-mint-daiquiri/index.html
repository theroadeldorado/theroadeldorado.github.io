Frozen Mint Daiquiri

Light rum, lime, a little sugar and a few fresh mint leaves whizzed with crushed ice. Old Mr. Boston's frozen cousin of the Mojito, cool and herbal without the soda.

Ingredients

  • 2 oz white rum
  • ½ oz fresh lime juice
  • 6 mint leaves
  • ½ oz simple syrup

Steps

  1. Blend 2 oz white rum, ½ oz fresh lime juice, 6 mint leaves and ½ oz simple syrup with ½ cup of ice until smooth, then pour into a rocks glass.
  2. Garnish with a sprig of mint.
