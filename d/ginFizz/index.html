Gin Fizz

Gin, lemon and sugar shaken hard and lengthened with soda, served without ice so you drink it while it fizzes.

Ingredients

  • 1½ oz gin
  • 1 oz fresh lemon juice
  • ¼ oz simple syrup
  • Soda water, to top

Steps

  1. Fill the shaker with ice.
  2. Add 1½ oz gin to the shaker.
  3. Add 1 oz fresh lemon juice to the shaker.
  4. Add ¼ oz simple syrup to the shaker.
  5. Cap the shaker and shake hard for about 12 seconds.
  6. Strain into a chilled highball glass.
  7. Top with soda water.
  8. Garnish with a lemon wheel on the rim.
