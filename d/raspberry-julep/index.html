Raspberry Julep

Mint pressed with raspberry syrup, then bourbon over a mound of crushed ice. The Kentucky julep with a berry accent: cool, fragrant and lightly fruity.

Ingredients

  • 8 mint leaves
  • ½ oz raspberry syrup
  • 2 oz bourbon

Steps

  1. Muddle 8 mint leaves in a rocks glass.
  2. Add crushed ice to the rocks glass.
  3. Add ½ oz raspberry syrup to the rocks glass.
  4. Add 2 oz bourbon to the rocks glass.
  5. Give it a gentle stir.
  6. Garnish with a sprig of mint. Add a straw.
