Grim Reaper

Coffee liqueur and overproof rum over ice with a dash of grenadine. Sweet, roasty and much stronger than it tastes.

Ingredients

  • 1 oz coffee liqueur
  • 1 oz overproof rum
  • ¼ oz pomegranate grenadine

Steps

  1. Add ice cubes to a rocks glass.
  2. Add 1 oz coffee liqueur to the rocks glass.
  3. Add 1 oz overproof rum to the rocks glass.
  4. Add ¼ oz pomegranate grenadine to the rocks glass.
  5. Give it a gentle stir.
