SHAKEN · COUPE

Pure Passion

Light rum shaken with passion fruit syrup, fresh lime and a dash of peach bitters, served up. A tropical daiquiri riff: tangy, perfumed and stone-fruit soft.

Ingredients

  • 2 oz white rum
  • ¾ oz passion fruit syrup
  • ¾ oz fresh lime juice
  • 1 dash peach bitters

Steps

  1. Fill the shaker with ice.
  2. Add 2 oz white rum to the shaker.
  3. Add ¾ oz passion fruit syrup to the shaker.
  4. Add ¾ oz fresh lime juice to the shaker.
  5. Add 1 dash peach bitters to the shaker.
  6. Cap the shaker and shake hard for about 12 seconds.
  7. Strain into a chilled coupe.
  8. Garnish with a sprig of mint.
