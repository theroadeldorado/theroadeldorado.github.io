Banana Daiquiri

Light rum blended with ripe banana, banana liqueur, lime and a little sugar until smooth and icy. Lots of real banana, without the syrupy sweetness.

Ingredients

  • ½ banana
  • 1½ oz white rum
  • 1 oz crème de banane
  • ¼ oz fresh lime juice
  • ¼ oz simple syrup

Steps

  1. Blend ½ banana, 1½ oz white rum, 1 oz crème de banane, ¼ oz fresh lime juice and ¼ oz simple syrup with ½ cup of ice until smooth, then pour into a coupe.
  2. Drop in a cocktail cherry.
