SHAKEN · COUPE

Jack Rose

Applejack shaken with fresh lime and real pomegranate grenadine. A crisp, tart apple-brandy sour from the Savoy book, and the drink Jake Barnes orders in The Sun Also Rises.

Ingredients

  • ¾ oz fresh lime juice
  • ¾ oz pomegranate grenadine
  • 2 oz applejack

Steps

  1. Fill the shaker with ice.
  2. Add ¾ oz fresh lime juice to the shaker.
  3. Add ¾ oz pomegranate grenadine to the shaker.
  4. Add 2 oz applejack to the shaker.
  5. Cap the shaker and shake hard for about 12 seconds.
  6. Strain into a chilled coupe.
  7. Express a lemon peel over the drink and drop it in.
