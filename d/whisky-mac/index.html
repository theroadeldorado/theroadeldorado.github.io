Whisky Mac

Scotch and ginger wine over ice. A Scottish winter warmer, said to be named for a Colonel Macdonald who mixed it in India: smooth malt with a gentle, peppery ginger heat.

Ingredients

  • 1½ oz blended Scotch
  • 1 oz ginger wine

Steps

  1. Add a big ice cube to a rocks glass.
  2. Add 1½ oz blended Scotch to the rocks glass.
  3. Add 1 oz ginger wine to the rocks glass.
  4. Give it a gentle stir.
