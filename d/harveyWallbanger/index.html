Harvey Wallbanger

A Screwdriver crowned with a float of Galliano.

Ingredients

  • 1 oz vodka
  • 3 oz fresh orange juice
  • ½ oz Galliano, floated on top

Steps

  1. Add ice cubes to a highball glass.
  2. Add 1 oz vodka to the highball glass.
  3. Add 3 oz fresh orange juice to the highball glass.
  4. Give it a gentle stir.
  5. Over the back of a spoon, gently pour the Galliano onto the drink so it floats on top.
  6. Garnish with an orange wheel on the rim.
