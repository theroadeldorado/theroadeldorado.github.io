SHAKEN · COUPE

Poppy

Two parts gin to one of crème de cacao, shaken cold and served up. A Savoy-era after-dinner drink: juniper first, then a gentle chocolate sweetness.

Ingredients

  • 1½ oz gin
  • ¾ oz crème de cacao

Steps

  1. Fill the shaker with ice.
  2. Add 1½ oz gin to the shaker.
  3. Add ¾ oz crème de cacao to the shaker.
  4. Cap the shaker and shake hard for about 12 seconds.
  5. Strain into a chilled coupe.
