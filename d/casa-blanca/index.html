Casa Blanca

Light rum lifted by small measures of lime, orange liqueur and maraschino, as Mr. Boston printed it. Dry, clean and quietly fruity.

Ingredients

  • 2 oz white rum
  • ¼ oz fresh lime juice
  • ¼ oz Cointreau
  • ¼ oz maraschino liqueur

Steps

  1. Fill the shaker with ice.
  2. Add 2 oz white rum to the shaker.
  3. Add ¼ oz fresh lime juice to the shaker.
  4. Add ¼ oz Cointreau to the shaker.
  5. Add ¼ oz maraschino liqueur to the shaker.
  6. Cap the shaker and shake hard for about 12 seconds.
  7. Strain into a chilled coupe.
