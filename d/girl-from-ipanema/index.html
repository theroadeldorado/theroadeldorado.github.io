SHAKEN · FLUTE

Girl from Ipanema

Cachaça shaken with lemon and agave, then topped with Champagne. A Brazilian answer to the French 75, grassy and bright with plenty of fizz.

Ingredients

  • 1 oz cachaça
  • ½ oz fresh lemon juice
  • ¼ oz agave syrup
  • 3 oz Champagne

Steps

  1. Fill the shaker with ice.
  2. Add 1 oz cachaça to the shaker.
  3. Add ½ oz fresh lemon juice to the shaker.
  4. Add ¼ oz agave syrup to the shaker.
  5. Cap the shaker and shake hard for about 12 seconds.
  6. Strain into a chilled flute.
  7. Top with 3 oz Champagne.
