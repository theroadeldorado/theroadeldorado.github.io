Rum Cobbler

Dark rum, a touch of sugar and a splash of soda, stirred over a glass packed with crushed ice and dressed with fruit. The old Mr. Boston cobbler: cold, lightly sweet and all about the rum.

Ingredients

  • ¼ oz simple syrup
  • 2 oz soda water
  • 2 oz dark rum

Steps

  1. Add crushed ice to a highball glass.
  2. Add ¼ oz simple syrup to the highball glass.
  3. Add 2 oz soda water to the highball glass.
  4. Add 2 oz dark rum to the highball glass.
  5. Give it a gentle stir.
  6. Garnish with an orange slice. Add a straw.
