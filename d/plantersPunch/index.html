Planter's Punch

Dark rum, fresh lemon, orange and pineapple with a few dashes of bitters. Jamaica's house punch, by the glass.

Ingredients

  • 1½ oz dark rum
  • ¾ oz fresh lemon juice
  • ½ oz simple syrup
  • 1¼ oz fresh orange juice
  • 1¼ oz pineapple juice
  • ¼ oz pomegranate grenadine
  • 3 dashes Angostura bitters

Steps

  1. Fill the shaker with ice. Add ice cubes to a highball glass.
  2. Add 1½ oz dark rum to the shaker.
  3. Add ¾ oz fresh lemon juice to the shaker.
  4. Add ½ oz simple syrup to the shaker.
  5. Add 1¼ oz fresh orange juice to the shaker.
  6. Add 1¼ oz pineapple juice to the shaker.
  7. Add ¼ oz pomegranate grenadine to the shaker.
  8. Add 3 dashes Angostura bitters to the shaker.
  9. Cap the shaker and shake hard for about 12 seconds.
  10. Strain into the highball glass.
  11. Express an orange peel over the drink and drop it in. Add a straw.
