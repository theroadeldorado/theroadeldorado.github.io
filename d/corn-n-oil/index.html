STIRRED · ROCKS

Corn 'n' Oil

Aged Barbados-style rum stirred with falernum and plenty of bitters over crushed ice, under a float of blackstrap rum. Spiced with clove and lime, and rich as molasses.

Ingredients

  • 2 oz aged rum
  • ½ oz falernum
  • 3 dashes Angostura bitters
  • ¼ oz dark rum, floated on top

Steps

  1. Fill the mixing glass with ice. Add crushed ice to a rocks glass.
  2. Add 2 oz aged rum to the mixing glass.
  3. Add ½ oz falernum to the mixing glass.
  4. Add 3 dashes Angostura bitters to the mixing glass.
  5. Stir with a bar spoon for about 30 seconds, until well chilled.
  6. Strain into the rocks glass.
  7. Over the back of a spoon, gently pour ¼ oz dark rum onto the drink so it sits on top.
  8. Garnish with a lime wedge on the rim.
