Imperial Fizz

Rye and a little light rum shaken with lemon and sugar, then lifted with soda over ice. An Old Mr. Boston fizz: crisp, gently tart and drier than a Collins.

Ingredients

  • ¾ oz fresh lemon juice
  • ½ oz white rum
  • 1½ oz rye whiskey
  • ½ oz simple syrup
  • Soda water, to top

Steps

  1. Fill the shaker with ice. Add ice cubes to a highball glass.
  2. Add ¾ oz fresh lemon juice to the shaker.
  3. Add ½ oz white rum to the shaker.
  4. Add 1½ oz rye whiskey to the shaker.
  5. Add ½ oz simple syrup to the shaker.
  6. Cap the shaker and shake hard for about 12 seconds.
  7. Strain into the highball glass.
  8. Top with soda water.
