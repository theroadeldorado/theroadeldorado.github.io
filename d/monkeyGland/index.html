Monkey Gland

Gin and fresh orange juice with a whisper of absinthe and pomegranate grenadine.

Ingredients

  • 2 oz gin
  • 1 oz fresh orange juice
  • ¼ oz pomegranate grenadine
  • ½ barspoon absinthe

Steps

  1. Fill the shaker with ice.
  2. Add 2 oz gin to the shaker.
  3. Add 1 oz fresh orange juice to the shaker.
  4. Add ¼ oz pomegranate grenadine to the shaker.
  5. Add ½ barspoon absinthe to the shaker.
  6. Cap the shaker and shake hard for about 12 seconds.
  7. Strain into a chilled coupe.
  8. Express an orange peel over the drink and drop it in.
