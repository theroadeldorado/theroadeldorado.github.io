SHAKEN · COUPE

Boxcar

Gin and orange liqueur with fresh lemon and a little pomegranate grenadine, shaken silky with egg white. Smooth and orange-forward, with a soft foam.

Ingredients

  • 1½ oz gin
  • ¾ oz Cointreau
  • ¾ oz fresh lemon juice
  • ¼ oz pomegranate grenadine
  • ¾ oz egg white

Steps

  1. Fill the shaker with ice.
  2. Add 1½ oz gin to the shaker.
  3. Add ¾ oz Cointreau to the shaker.
  4. Add ¾ oz fresh lemon juice to the shaker.
  5. Add ¼ oz pomegranate grenadine to the shaker.
  6. Add ¾ oz egg white to the shaker.
  7. Cap the shaker and shake hard for about 12 seconds.
  8. Strain into a chilled coupe.
