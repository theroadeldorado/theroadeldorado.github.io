Paloma

Tequila and grapefruit soda with a squeeze of lime.

Ingredients

  • 2 oz blanco tequila
  • ½ oz fresh lime juice
  • 4 oz grapefruit soda

Steps

  1. Add ice cubes to a highball glass.
  2. Add 2 oz blanco tequila to the highball glass.
  3. Add ½ oz fresh lime juice to the highball glass.
  4. Top with 4 oz grapefruit soda.
  5. Give it a gentle stir.
  6. Garnish with a lime wedge on the rim.
