Rum Milk Punch

Light rum shaken hard with cold milk and a little sugar, then dusted with nutmeg. A mild, creamy brunch drink from the old Mr. Boston guides, gentler than eggnog and just as soothing.

Ingredients

  • ¼ oz simple syrup
  • 2 oz white rum
  • 5 oz milk

Steps

  1. Fill the shaker with ice.
  2. Add ¼ oz simple syrup to the shaker.
  3. Add 2 oz white rum to the shaker.
  4. Add 5 oz milk to the shaker.
  5. Cap the shaker and shake hard for about 12 seconds.
  6. Strain into a chilled highball glass.
  7. Grate a little nutmeg over the top.
