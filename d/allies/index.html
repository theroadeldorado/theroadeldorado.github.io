STIRRED · COUPE

Allies

Gin and dry vermouth in equal parts with a touch of caraway-scented kümmel. A dry Savoy aperitif named for the First World War alliance.

Ingredients

  • 1½ oz gin
  • 1½ oz dry vermouth
  • ¼ oz kümmel

Steps

  1. Fill the mixing glass with ice.
  2. Add 1½ oz gin to the mixing glass.
  3. Add 1½ oz dry vermouth to the mixing glass.
  4. Add ¼ oz kümmel to the mixing glass.
  5. Stir with a bar spoon for about 30 seconds, until well chilled.
  6. Strain into a chilled coupe.
  7. Express a lemon peel over the drink and drop it in.
