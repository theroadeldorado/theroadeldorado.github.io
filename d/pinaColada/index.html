Piña Colada

Rum, pineapple and coconut blended smooth.

Ingredients

  • 2 oz white rum
  • 3 oz pineapple juice
  • 1½ oz cream of coconut

Steps

  1. Blend 2 oz white rum, 3 oz pineapple juice and 1½ oz cream of coconut with a cup of ice until smooth, then pour into a hurricane glass.
  2. Garnish with a pineapple wedge and a cherry. Add a straw.
