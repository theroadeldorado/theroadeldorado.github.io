Figgy Thyme

A ripe fig and thyme crushed with honey, then vodka, bitters and tonic over ice. Jammy and herbal, with a bitter edge from the quinine.

Ingredients

  • 1 fig
  • 1 thyme sprig
  • ¼ oz honey syrup
  • 2 oz vodka
  • 2 dashes Angostura bitters
  • Tonic water, to top

Steps

  1. Muddle 1 fig in a highball glass.
  2. Muddle 1 thyme sprig in the highball glass.
  3. Add ice cubes to the highball glass.
  4. Add ¼ oz honey syrup to the highball glass.
  5. Add 2 oz vodka to the highball glass.
  6. Add 2 dashes Angostura bitters to the highball glass.
  7. Top with tonic water.
  8. Give it a gentle stir.
