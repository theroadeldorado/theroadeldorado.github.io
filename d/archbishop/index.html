STIRRED · ROCKS

Archbishop

Gin stirred with green ginger wine and a spoonful of Bénédictine, served over a big cube. Warming ginger and honeyed herbs over a dry gin backbone.

Ingredients

  • 2 oz gin
  • 1 oz ginger wine
  • ¼ oz Bénédictine

Steps

  1. Fill the mixing glass with ice. Add a big ice cube to a rocks glass.
  2. Add 2 oz gin to the mixing glass.
  3. Add 1 oz ginger wine to the mixing glass.
  4. Add ¼ oz Bénédictine to the mixing glass.
  5. Stir with a bar spoon for about 30 seconds, until well chilled.
  6. Strain into the rocks glass.
  7. Garnish with a lime wedge on the rim.
