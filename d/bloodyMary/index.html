Bloody Mary

Vodka and seasoned tomato juice. The brunch classic.

Ingredients

  • 2 oz vodka
  • 4 oz tomato juice
  • ½ oz fresh lemon juice
  • 2 dashes Worcestershire sauce
  • 2 dashes hot sauce (optional)

Steps

  1. Wet the rim of a highball glass and roll it in salt.
  2. Add ice cubes to the highball glass.
  3. Add 2 oz vodka to the highball glass.
  4. Add 4 oz tomato juice to the highball glass.
  5. Add ½ oz fresh lemon juice to the highball glass.
  6. Add 2 dashes Worcestershire sauce to the highball glass.
  7. Add 2 dashes hot sauce (optional) to the highball glass.
  8. Give it a gentle stir.
  9. Garnish with a celery stalk.
