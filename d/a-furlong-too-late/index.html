A Furlong Too Late

Light rum and spicy ginger beer over ice with a twist of lemon. A simple racetrack highball, for the horse that came up one furlong short.

Ingredients

  • 2 oz white rum
  • 4 oz ginger beer

Steps

  1. Add ice cubes to a highball glass.
  2. Add 2 oz white rum to the highball glass.
  3. Top with 4 oz ginger beer.
  4. Give it a gentle stir.
  5. Express a lemon peel over the drink and drop it in.
