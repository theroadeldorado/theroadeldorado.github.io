Gin and Soda

Gin, soda and a squeeze of lime over ice. The driest, lightest way to drink gin: all juniper and bubbles, nothing sweet.

Ingredients

  • 2 oz gin
  • ¼ oz fresh lime juice
  • 5 oz soda water

Steps

  1. Add ice cubes to a highball glass.
  2. Add 2 oz gin to the highball glass.
  3. Add ¼ oz fresh lime juice to the highball glass.
  4. Top with 5 oz soda water.
  5. Give it a gentle stir.
  6. Garnish with a lime wedge on the rim.
