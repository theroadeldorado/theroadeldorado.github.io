SHAKEN · COUPE

Stone Sour

Apricot brandy shaken with fresh orange and lemon and served up. Orange juice softens the sour, so it drinks like apricot nectar with a bright, tangy finish.

Ingredients

  • 1½ oz apricot brandy
  • 1 oz fresh orange juice
  • ¾ oz fresh lemon juice
  • ¼ oz simple syrup

Steps

  1. Fill the shaker with ice.
  2. Add 1½ oz apricot brandy to the shaker.
  3. Add 1 oz fresh orange juice to the shaker.
  4. Add ¾ oz fresh lemon juice to the shaker.
  5. Add ¼ oz simple syrup to the shaker.
  6. Cap the shaker and shake hard for about 12 seconds.
  7. Strain into a chilled coupe.
  8. Garnish with an orange slice.
