Dubonnet Cocktail

Dubonnet and gin in equal measure, stirred cold, as the Savoy lists it. Wine-rich and lightly bitter with quinine, and long a royal favourite.

Ingredients

  • 1½ oz Dubonnet
  • 1½ oz gin

Steps

  1. Fill the mixing glass with ice.
  2. Add 1½ oz Dubonnet to the mixing glass.
  3. Add 1½ oz gin to the mixing glass.
  4. Stir with a bar spoon for about 30 seconds, until well chilled.
  5. Strain into a chilled coupe.
  6. Express a lemon peel over the drink and drop it in.
