Irish Spring

Irish whiskey shaken with peach, fresh orange and lemon, then poured long over ice. Soft, fruity and bright, with a little peach sweetness behind the whiskey.

Ingredients

  • 1½ oz Irish whiskey
  • ½ oz peach schnapps
  • 1 oz fresh orange juice
  • ¾ oz fresh lemon juice
  • ¼ oz simple syrup

Steps

  1. Fill the shaker with ice. Add ice cubes to a highball glass.
  2. Add 1½ oz Irish whiskey to the shaker.
  3. Add ½ oz peach schnapps to the shaker.
  4. Add 1 oz fresh orange juice to the shaker.
  5. Add ¾ oz fresh lemon juice to the shaker.
  6. Add ¼ oz simple syrup to the shaker.
  7. Cap the shaker and shake hard for about 12 seconds.
  8. Strain into the highball glass.
  9. Garnish with an orange slice.
