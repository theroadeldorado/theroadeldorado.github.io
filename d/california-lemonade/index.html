California Lemonade

Whiskey shaken with lemon, lime, sugar and a touch of grenadine, then lengthened with soda over crushed ice. Old Mr. Boston's grown-up lemonade for a hot afternoon.

Ingredients

  • 2 oz bourbon
  • 1 oz fresh lemon juice
  • ¾ oz fresh lime juice
  • ¾ oz simple syrup
  • ¼ oz pomegranate grenadine
  • Soda water, to top

Steps

  1. Fill the shaker with ice. Add crushed ice to a highball glass.
  2. Add 2 oz bourbon to the shaker.
  3. Add 1 oz fresh lemon juice to the shaker.
  4. Add ¾ oz fresh lime juice to the shaker.
  5. Add ¾ oz simple syrup to the shaker.
  6. Add ¼ oz pomegranate grenadine to the shaker.
  7. Cap the shaker and shake hard for about 12 seconds.
  8. Strain into the highball glass.
  9. Top with soda water.
  10. Garnish with a lemon wheel on the rim. Add a straw.
