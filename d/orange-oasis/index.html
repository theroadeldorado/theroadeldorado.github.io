SHAKEN · HIGHBALL

Orange Oasis

Gin, fresh orange juice and a little cherry liqueur shaken and poured over ice, then topped with ginger ale. Juicy and gently spiced, a long brunch drink with a cherry finish.

Ingredients

  • ½ oz cherry liqueur
  • 1½ oz gin
  • 4 oz fresh orange juice
  • Ginger ale, to top

Steps

  1. Fill the shaker with ice. Add ice cubes to a highball glass.
  2. Add ½ oz cherry liqueur to the shaker.
  3. Add 1½ oz gin to the shaker.
  4. Add 4 oz fresh orange juice to the shaker.
  5. Cap the shaker and shake hard for about 12 seconds.
  6. Strain into the highball glass.
  7. Top with ginger ale.
  8. Garnish with an orange wheel on the rim.
