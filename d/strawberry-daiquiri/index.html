Strawberry Daiquiri

Ripe strawberries crushed in the shaker with light rum, fresh lime and sugar, then shaken hard and double strained. Cuba's Daiquiri de Fresa: tart and juicy, and it tastes of real fruit rather than syrup.

Ingredients

  • 5 strawberries
  • 2 oz white rum
  • ¾ oz fresh lime juice
  • ¾ oz simple syrup

Steps

  1. Muddle 5 strawberries in the shaker.
  2. Fill the shaker with ice.
  3. Add 2 oz white rum to the shaker.
  4. Add ¾ oz fresh lime juice to the shaker.
  5. Add ¾ oz simple syrup to the shaker.
  6. Cap the shaker and shake hard for about 12 seconds.
  7. Strain into a chilled coupe.
  8. Garnish with a strawberry and a sprig of mint.
