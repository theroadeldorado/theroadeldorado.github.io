Dirty Martini

A vodka martini clouded with a splash of olive brine. Savory and salty.

Ingredients

  • 2 oz vodka
  • ¼ oz dry vermouth
  • ¼ oz olive brine

Steps

  1. Fill the mixing glass with ice.
  2. Add 2 oz vodka to the mixing glass.
  3. Add ¼ oz dry vermouth to the mixing glass.
  4. Add ¼ oz olive brine to the mixing glass.
  5. Stir with a bar spoon for about 30 seconds, until well chilled.
  6. Strain into a chilled martini glass.
  7. Garnish with olives on a pick.
