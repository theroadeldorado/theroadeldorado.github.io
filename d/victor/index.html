STIRRED · COUPE

Victor

Sweet vermouth stirred with gin and brandy, two parts to one and one, as the Savoy prints it. Rich and mellow: the vermouth leads, the gin brightens it and the brandy warms the finish.

Ingredients

  • 1½ oz sweet vermouth
  • ¾ oz gin
  • ¾ oz cognac

Steps

  1. Fill the mixing glass with ice.
  2. Add 1½ oz sweet vermouth to the mixing glass.
  3. Add ¾ oz gin to the mixing glass.
  4. Add ¾ oz cognac to the mixing glass.
  5. Stir with a bar spoon for about 30 seconds, until well chilled.
  6. Strain into a chilled coupe.
