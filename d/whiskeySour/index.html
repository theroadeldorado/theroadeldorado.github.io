SHAKEN · ROCKS

Whiskey Sour

Bourbon, lemon and sugar, with egg white for a silky top if you like.

Ingredients

  • 2 oz bourbon
  • ¾ oz fresh lemon juice
  • ¾ oz simple syrup
  • ¾ oz egg white (optional)

Steps

  1. Fill the shaker with ice. Add ice cubes to a rocks glass.
  2. Add 2 oz bourbon to the shaker.
  3. Add ¾ oz fresh lemon juice to the shaker.
  4. Add ¾ oz simple syrup to the shaker.
  5. Add ¾ oz egg white (optional) to the shaker.
  6. Cap the shaker and shake hard for about 12 seconds.
  7. Strain into the rocks glass.
  8. Garnish with a cherry and an orange slice.
