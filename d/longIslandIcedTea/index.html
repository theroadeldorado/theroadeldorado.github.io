Long Island Iced Tea

Five spirits, fresh lemon and a splash of cola. There's no tea in it, and it's stronger than it tastes.

Ingredients

  • ½ oz vodka
  • ½ oz blanco tequila
  • ½ oz white rum
  • ½ oz gin
  • ½ oz Cointreau
  • ¾ oz fresh lemon juice
  • 1 oz simple syrup
  • Cola, to top, just a splash

Steps

  1. Add ice cubes to a highball glass.
  2. Add ½ oz vodka to the highball glass.
  3. Add ½ oz blanco tequila to the highball glass.
  4. Add ½ oz white rum to the highball glass.
  5. Add ½ oz gin to the highball glass.
  6. Add ½ oz Cointreau to the highball glass.
  7. Add ¾ oz fresh lemon juice to the highball glass.
  8. Add 1 oz simple syrup to the highball glass.
  9. Top with cola (just a splash).
  10. Give it a gentle stir.
  11. Garnish with a lemon wheel on the rim. Add a straw.
