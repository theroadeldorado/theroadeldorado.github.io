Port Wine Cocktail

Ruby port stirred briefly over ice with a splash of brandy and served up. A Savoy-era aperitif that tastes like plums and berries, with just enough warmth to call it a cocktail.

Ingredients

  • 2½ oz ruby port
  • ¼ oz cognac

Steps

  1. Fill the mixing glass with ice.
  2. Add 2½ oz ruby port to the mixing glass.
  3. Add ¼ oz cognac to the mixing glass.
  4. Stir with a bar spoon for about 30 seconds, until well chilled.
  5. Strain into a chilled coupe.
  6. Express an orange peel over the drink and drop it in.
