Amaretto Rose

Amaretto and fresh lime topped with soda over ice. A tall, nutty spritz that drinks much drier than amaretto alone.

Ingredients

  • 1½ oz amaretto
  • ½ oz fresh lime juice
  • Soda water, to top

Steps

  1. Add ice cubes to a highball glass.
  2. Add 1½ oz amaretto to the highball glass.
  3. Add ½ oz fresh lime juice to the highball glass.
  4. Top with soda water.
  5. Give it a gentle stir.
  6. Garnish with a lime wedge on the rim.
