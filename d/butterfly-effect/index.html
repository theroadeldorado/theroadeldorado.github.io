SHAKEN · HURRICANE

Butterfly Effect

Vodka, raspberry, cranberry and a splash of blue curaçao, shaken with lemon and lime and served long. Berry-tart, juicy and very easy going.

Ingredients

  • 1¾ oz vodka
  • ¼ oz raspberry syrup
  • ¾ oz cranberry juice
  • ½ oz fresh lemon juice
  • ¼ oz blue curaçao
  • ½ oz simple syrup
  • ¼ oz fresh lime juice

Steps

  1. Fill the shaker with ice. Add ice cubes to a hurricane glass.
  2. Add 1¾ oz vodka to the shaker.
  3. Add ¼ oz raspberry syrup to the shaker.
  4. Add ¾ oz cranberry juice to the shaker.
  5. Add ½ oz fresh lemon juice to the shaker.
  6. Add ¼ oz blue curaçao to the shaker.
  7. Add ½ oz simple syrup to the shaker.
  8. Add ¼ oz fresh lime juice to the shaker.
  9. Cap the shaker and shake hard for about 12 seconds.
  10. Strain into the hurricane glass.
  11. Garnish with a sprig of mint. Add a straw.
