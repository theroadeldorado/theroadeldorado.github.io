Champagne Cocktail

A sugar cube soaked in bitters and a splash of cognac, with Champagne poured over to set it fizzing.

Ingredients

  • 1 sugar cube
  • 2 dashes Angostura bitters
  • ¼ oz cognac
  • Champagne, to top

Steps

  1. Drop a sugar cube into a flute and soak it with the Angostura bitters.
  2. Add ¼ oz cognac to the flute.
  3. Top with Champagne.
  4. Garnish with an orange peel and a cherry.
