Moscow Mule

Vodka, lime and spicy ginger beer.

Ingredients

  • 2 oz vodka
  • ½ oz fresh lime juice
  • 4 oz ginger beer

Steps

  1. Add ice cubes to a highball glass.
  2. Add 2 oz vodka to the highball glass.
  3. Add ½ oz fresh lime juice to the highball glass.
  4. Top with 4 oz ginger beer.
  5. Give it a gentle stir.
  6. Garnish with a lime wedge on the rim.
