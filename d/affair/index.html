BUILT · HIGHBALL

Affair

Strawberry liqueur with orange and cranberry juice over ice, lifted with soda. A light, berry-sweet long drink for a summer afternoon.

Ingredients

  • 1½ oz strawberry liqueur
  • 2 oz fresh orange juice
  • 2 oz cranberry juice
  • Soda water, to top

Steps

  1. Add ice cubes to a highball glass.
  2. Add 1½ oz strawberry liqueur to the highball glass.
  3. Add 2 oz fresh orange juice to the highball glass.
  4. Add 2 oz cranberry juice to the highball glass.
  5. Top with soda water.
  6. Give it a gentle stir.
