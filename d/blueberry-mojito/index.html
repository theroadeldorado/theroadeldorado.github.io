Blueberry Mojito

Mint and fresh blueberries crushed with lime and sugar, then dark rum, crushed ice and a fizzy top. Jammy berry fruit with a cool, minty finish.

Ingredients

  • 8 mint leaves
  • 8 blueberries
  • 1 oz fresh lime juice
  • ¾ oz simple syrup
  • 2 oz dark rum
  • ¼ oz fresh lemon juice
  • Soda water, to top

Steps

  1. Muddle 8 mint leaves in a highball glass.
  2. Muddle 8 blueberries in the highball glass.
  3. Add crushed ice to the highball glass.
  4. Add 1 oz fresh lime juice to the highball glass.
  5. Add ¾ oz simple syrup to the highball glass.
  6. Add 2 oz dark rum to the highball glass.
  7. Add ¼ oz fresh lemon juice to the highball glass.
  8. Top with soda water.
  9. Give it a gentle stir.
  10. Garnish with a sprig of mint. Add a straw.
