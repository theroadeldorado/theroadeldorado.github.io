Americano

Campari and sweet vermouth stretched with soda. Bitter and refreshing.

Ingredients

  • 1½ oz Campari
  • 1½ oz sweet vermouth
  • Soda water, to top

Steps

  1. Add ice cubes to a highball glass.
  2. Add 1½ oz Campari to the highball glass.
  3. Add 1½ oz sweet vermouth to the highball glass.
  4. Top with soda water.
  5. Give it a gentle stir.
  6. Garnish with an orange slice. Add a stirrer.
