Negroni

Equal parts gin, Campari and sweet vermouth: bitter, bright and built for sipping.

Ingredients

  • 1 oz gin
  • 1 oz Campari
  • 1 oz sweet vermouth

Steps

  1. Fill the mixing glass with ice. Add a big ice cube to a rocks glass.
  2. Add 1 oz gin to the mixing glass.
  3. Add 1 oz Campari to the mixing glass.
  4. Add 1 oz sweet vermouth to the mixing glass.
  5. Stir with a bar spoon for about 30 seconds, until well chilled.
  6. Strain into the rocks glass.
  7. Express an orange peel over the drink and drop it in.
