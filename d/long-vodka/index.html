Long Vodka

Vodka, fresh lime and a generous shake of Angostura over ice, topped with tonic. The bitters bring warm spice and the quinine keeps it crisp and dry.

Ingredients

  • 1¾ oz vodka
  • ½ oz fresh lime juice
  • 4 dashes Angostura bitters
  • 4 oz tonic water

Steps

  1. Add ice cubes to a highball glass.
  2. Add 1¾ oz vodka to the highball glass.
  3. Add ½ oz fresh lime juice to the highball glass.
  4. Add 4 dashes Angostura bitters to the highball glass.
  5. Top with 4 oz tonic water.
  6. Give it a gentle stir.
  7. Garnish with a lime wedge on the rim.
