Brandy Flip

Brandy shaken hard with a whole egg and a little sugar, then dusted with nutmeg. Jerry Thomas's cold flip: rich, velvety and gently spiced.

Ingredients

  • 2 oz cognac
  • 1½ oz beaten egg
  • ¼ oz simple syrup

Steps

  1. Fill the shaker with ice.
  2. Add 2 oz cognac to the shaker.
  3. Add 1½ oz beaten egg to the shaker.
  4. Add ¼ oz simple syrup to the shaker.
  5. Cap the shaker and shake hard for about 12 seconds.
  6. Strain into a chilled coupe.
  7. Grate a little nutmeg over the top.
