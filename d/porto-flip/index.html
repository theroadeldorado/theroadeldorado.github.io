SHAKEN · COUPE

Porto Flip

Ruby port and a little brandy shaken with an egg yolk, served up under fresh nutmeg. The IBA's silky nightcap: plummy, velvety and not too sweet.

Ingredients

  • ½ oz cognac
  • 1½ oz ruby port
  • ½ oz beaten egg, the yolk only

Steps

  1. Fill the shaker with ice.
  2. Add ½ oz cognac to the shaker.
  3. Add 1½ oz ruby port to the shaker.
  4. Add ½ oz beaten egg (the yolk only) to the shaker.
  5. Cap the shaker and shake hard for about 12 seconds.
  6. Strain into a chilled coupe.
  7. Grate a little nutmeg over the top.
