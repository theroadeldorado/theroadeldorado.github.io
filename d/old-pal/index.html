STIRRED · NICK & NORA

Old Pal

Rye, dry vermouth and Campari in equal parts, stirred and served up. Harry McElhone printed it in 1922 for a Paris sportswriter friend: a lean, bitter Boulevardier.

Ingredients

  • 1 oz rye whiskey
  • 1 oz dry vermouth
  • 1 oz Campari

Steps

  1. Fill the mixing glass with ice.
  2. Add 1 oz rye whiskey to the mixing glass.
  3. Add 1 oz dry vermouth to the mixing glass.
  4. Add 1 oz Campari to the mixing glass.
  5. Stir with a bar spoon for about 30 seconds, until well chilled.
  6. Strain into a chilled Nick & Nora glass.
  7. Express a lemon peel over the drink and drop it in.
