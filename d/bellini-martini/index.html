Bellini Martini

Vodka shaken with peach purée and peach schnapps, served straight up with a lemon twist. All the stone fruit of a Bellini with none of the bubbles.

Ingredients

  • 1½ oz vodka
  • ¾ oz peach purée
  • ¾ oz peach schnapps

Steps

  1. Fill the shaker with ice.
  2. Add 1½ oz vodka to the shaker.
  3. Add ¾ oz peach purée to the shaker.
  4. Add ¾ oz peach schnapps to the shaker.
  5. Cap the shaker and shake hard for about 12 seconds.
  6. Strain into a chilled martini glass.
  7. Express a lemon peel over the drink and drop it in.
