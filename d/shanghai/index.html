SHAKEN · COUPE

Shanghai

Jamaican rum, lemon and grenadine with a quiet thread of anise, from the Savoy. Funky rum up front, tart fruit in the middle and a licorice note on the finish.

Ingredients

  • ¾ oz fresh lemon juice
  • ¼ oz pastis
  • 2 oz dark rum
  • ½ oz pomegranate grenadine

Steps

  1. Fill the shaker with ice.
  2. Add ¾ oz fresh lemon juice to the shaker.
  3. Add ¼ oz pastis to the shaker.
  4. Add 2 oz dark rum to the shaker.
  5. Add ½ oz pomegranate grenadine to the shaker.
  6. Cap the shaker and shake hard for about 12 seconds.
  7. Strain into a chilled coupe.
