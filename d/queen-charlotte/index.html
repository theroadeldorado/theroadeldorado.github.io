Queen Charlotte

Claret over crushed ice with raspberry syrup and fresh lemon, filled up with soda. Charles H. Baker's wine cooler for a hot afternoon: berry-sweet, gently tart and low in alcohol.

Ingredients

  • 3 oz red wine
  • 1 oz raspberry syrup
  • ¾ oz fresh lemon juice
  • 3 oz soda water

Steps

  1. Add crushed ice to a highball glass.
  2. Add 3 oz red wine to the highball glass.
  3. Add 1 oz raspberry syrup to the highball glass.
  4. Add ¾ oz fresh lemon juice to the highball glass.
  5. Top with 3 oz soda water.
  6. Give it a gentle stir.
  7. Add a straw.
