STIRRED · ROCKS

The Strange Weaver

Light rum and gin share the base of this Negroni cousin, stirred with sweet vermouth, Campari and a touch each of lemon and orgeat. Bitter and boozy like its parent, with a softer almond edge.

Ingredients

  • 1 oz white rum
  • 1 oz gin
  • ¾ oz sweet vermouth
  • ¾ oz Campari
  • ¼ oz fresh lemon juice
  • ¼ oz orgeat

Steps

  1. Fill the mixing glass with ice. Add a big ice cube to a rocks glass.
  2. Add 1 oz white rum to the mixing glass.
  3. Add 1 oz gin to the mixing glass.
  4. Add ¾ oz sweet vermouth to the mixing glass.
  5. Add ¾ oz Campari to the mixing glass.
  6. Add ¼ oz fresh lemon juice to the mixing glass.
  7. Add ¼ oz orgeat to the mixing glass.
  8. Stir with a bar spoon for about 30 seconds, until well chilled.
  9. Strain into the rocks glass.
  10. Express an orange peel over the drink and drop it in.
