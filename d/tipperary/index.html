STIRRED · NICK & NORA

Tipperary

Irish whiskey stirred with sweet vermouth, green Chartreuse and two dashes of bitters. A century-old Irish cousin of the Bijou: rich and herbal, with the whiskey's soft malt keeping the Chartreuse in line.

Ingredients

  • 2 oz Irish whiskey
  • 1 oz sweet vermouth
  • ½ oz Green Chartreuse
  • 2 dashes Angostura bitters

Steps

  1. Fill the mixing glass with ice.
  2. Add 2 oz Irish whiskey to the mixing glass.
  3. Add 1 oz sweet vermouth to the mixing glass.
  4. Add ½ oz Green Chartreuse to the mixing glass.
  5. Add 2 dashes Angostura bitters to the mixing glass.
  6. Stir with a bar spoon for about 30 seconds, until well chilled.
  7. Strain into a chilled Nick & Nora glass.
  8. Garnish with an orange slice.
