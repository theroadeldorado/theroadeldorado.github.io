SHAKEN · ROCKS

Gin Basil Smash

A fistful of basil muddled and shaken hard with gin, lemon and sugar. Jörg Meyer's 2008 Hamburg hit, first called the Gin Pesto: bright, peppery and herbal.

Ingredients

  • 12 basil leaves
  • 2 oz gin
  • ¾ oz fresh lemon juice
  • ½ oz simple syrup

Steps

  1. Muddle 12 basil leaves in the shaker.
  2. Fill the shaker with ice.
  3. Add 2 oz gin to the shaker.
  4. Add ¾ oz fresh lemon juice to the shaker.
  5. Add ½ oz simple syrup to the shaker.
  6. Cap the shaker and shake hard for about 12 seconds.
  7. Strain into a chilled rocks glass.
  8. Garnish with a sprig of mint.
