SHAKEN · COUPE

Between the Sheets

A Sidecar with a second spirit: cognac and white rum share the glass with orange liqueur and lemon.

Ingredients

  • 1 oz cognac
  • 1 oz white rum
  • 1 oz Cointreau
  • ¾ oz fresh lemon juice

Steps

  1. Fill the shaker with ice.
  2. Add 1 oz cognac to the shaker.
  3. Add 1 oz white rum to the shaker.
  4. Add 1 oz Cointreau to the shaker.
  5. Add ¾ oz fresh lemon juice to the shaker.
  6. Cap the shaker and shake hard for about 12 seconds.
  7. Strain into a chilled coupe.
  8. Express a lemon peel over the drink and drop it in.
