SHAKEN · HURRICANE

Singapore Sling

Gin, cherry liqueur, Bénédictine and pineapple: the Raffles Hotel's long, fruity sling.

Ingredients

  • 1 oz gin
  • ½ oz cherry liqueur
  • ¼ oz Cointreau
  • ¼ oz Bénédictine
  • 4 oz pineapple juice
  • ½ oz fresh lime juice
  • ¼ oz pomegranate grenadine
  • 1 dash Angostura bitters

Steps

  1. Fill the shaker with ice. Add ice cubes to a hurricane glass.
  2. Add 1 oz gin to the shaker.
  3. Add ½ oz cherry liqueur to the shaker.
  4. Add ¼ oz Cointreau to the shaker.
  5. Add ¼ oz Bénédictine to the shaker.
  6. Add 4 oz pineapple juice to the shaker.
  7. Add ½ oz fresh lime juice to the shaker.
  8. Add ¼ oz pomegranate grenadine to the shaker.
  9. Add 1 dash Angostura bitters to the shaker.
  10. Cap the shaker and shake hard for about 12 seconds.
  11. Strain into the hurricane glass.
  12. Garnish with a pineapple wedge and a cherry. Add a straw.
