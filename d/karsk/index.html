BUILT · IRISH COFFEE

Karsk

Hot coffee spiked with a generous pour of clear spirit, the way it is drunk in rural Norway. Tradition says to add coffee until a coin in the cup disappears, then spirit until it shows again.

Ingredients

  • 4 oz hot coffee
  • 2 oz vodka

Steps

  1. Add 4 oz hot coffee to an Irish coffee glass.
  2. Add 2 oz vodka to the Irish coffee glass.
  3. Give it a gentle stir.
