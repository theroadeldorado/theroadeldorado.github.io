Rum Cooler

Light rum over ice with fresh lemon, a little sugar and plenty of soda, our fresh stand-in for lemon-lime pop. Crisp, lightly sweet and made for a hot afternoon.

Ingredients

  • 2 oz white rum
  • ½ oz fresh lemon juice
  • ½ oz simple syrup
  • Soda water, to top

Steps

  1. Add ice cubes to a highball glass.
  2. Add 2 oz white rum to the highball glass.
  3. Add ½ oz fresh lemon juice to the highball glass.
  4. Add ½ oz simple syrup to the highball glass.
  5. Top with soda water.
  6. Give it a gentle stir.
  7. Garnish with a lemon wheel on the rim.
