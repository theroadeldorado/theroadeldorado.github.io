STIRRED · MARTINI

Martini

Cold, crisp gin softened with a little dry vermouth.

Ingredients

  • 2½ oz gin
  • ½ oz dry vermouth
  • 1 dash orange bitters (optional)

Steps

  1. Fill the mixing glass with ice.
  2. Add 2½ oz gin to the mixing glass.
  3. Add ½ oz dry vermouth to the mixing glass.
  4. Add 1 dash orange bitters (optional) to the mixing glass.
  5. Stir with a bar spoon for about 30 seconds, until well chilled.
  6. Strain into a chilled martini glass.
  7. Garnish with olives on a pick.
