SHAKEN · COUPE

A1

Gin and Grand Marnier, two to one, with a squeeze of lemon and a touch of grenadine. A Savoy-era orange-scented sour that's strong and dry.

Ingredients

  • 2 oz gin
  • 1 oz Grand Marnier
  • ½ oz fresh lemon juice
  • ¼ oz pomegranate grenadine

Steps

  1. Fill the shaker with ice.
  2. Add 2 oz gin to the shaker.
  3. Add 1 oz Grand Marnier to the shaker.
  4. Add ½ oz fresh lemon juice to the shaker.
  5. Add ¼ oz pomegranate grenadine to the shaker.
  6. Cap the shaker and shake hard for about 12 seconds.
  7. Strain into a chilled coupe.
  8. Express a lemon peel over the drink and drop it in.
