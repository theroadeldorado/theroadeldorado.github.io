BUILT · HIGHBALL

501 Blue

Fresh blueberries crushed with vodka, curaçao and lemon, then stretched with soda. A tart berry cooler that's easy to drink on a hot afternoon.

Ingredients

  • 6 blueberries
  • 1½ oz vodka
  • ¾ oz blue curaçao
  • ¾ oz fresh lemon juice
  • ¼ oz simple syrup
  • Soda water, to top

Steps

  1. Muddle 6 blueberries in a highball glass.
  2. Add ice cubes to the highball glass.
  3. Add 1½ oz vodka to the highball glass.
  4. Add ¾ oz blue curaçao to the highball glass.
  5. Add ¾ oz fresh lemon juice to the highball glass.
  6. Add ¼ oz simple syrup to the highball glass.
  7. Top with soda water.
  8. Give it a gentle stir.
  9. Garnish with a lemon wheel on the rim.
