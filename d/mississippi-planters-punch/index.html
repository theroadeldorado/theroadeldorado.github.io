Mississippi Planters Punch

Brandy, bourbon and light rum shaken with lemon and sugar, then lengthened with soda over ice. An Old Mr. Boston punch with three spirits that still drinks light and tart.

Ingredients

  • ¾ oz simple syrup
  • 1 oz fresh lemon juice
  • ½ oz white rum
  • ½ oz bourbon
  • 1 oz cognac
  • Soda water, to top

Steps

  1. Fill the shaker with ice. Add ice cubes to a highball glass.
  2. Add ¾ oz simple syrup to the shaker.
  3. Add 1 oz fresh lemon juice to the shaker.
  4. Add ½ oz white rum to the shaker.
  5. Add ½ oz bourbon to the shaker.
  6. Add 1 oz cognac to the shaker.
  7. Cap the shaker and shake hard for about 12 seconds.
  8. Strain into the highball glass.
  9. Top with soda water.
  10. Garnish with an orange wheel on the rim.
