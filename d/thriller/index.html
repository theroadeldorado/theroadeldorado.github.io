SHAKEN · COUPE

Thriller

Scotch shaken with ginger wine and fresh orange juice and served up. A warming sipper with malt and a little smoke, a gentle ginger heat and a soft citrus sweetness.

Ingredients

  • 1½ oz blended Scotch
  • 1 oz ginger wine
  • 1 oz fresh orange juice

Steps

  1. Fill the shaker with ice.
  2. Add 1½ oz blended Scotch to the shaker.
  3. Add 1 oz ginger wine to the shaker.
  4. Add 1 oz fresh orange juice to the shaker.
  5. Cap the shaker and shake hard for about 12 seconds.
  6. Strain into a chilled coupe.
