Rum Screwdriver

Light rum and fresh orange juice over ice. The brunch standby with rum swapped in for vodka, which gives it a softer, sweeter, faintly tropical edge.

Ingredients

  • 1½ oz white rum
  • 3 oz fresh orange juice

Steps

  1. Add ice cubes to a highball glass.
  2. Add 1½ oz white rum to the highball glass.
  3. Add 3 oz fresh orange juice to the highball glass.
  4. Give it a gentle stir.
  5. Garnish with an orange wheel on the rim.
