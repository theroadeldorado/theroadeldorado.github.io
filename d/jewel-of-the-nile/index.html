STIRRED · COUPE

Jewel of the Nile

Gin stirred with both Chartreuses and served up. Herbal, honeyed and potent: the green brings the bite and the yellow the softness.

Ingredients

  • 1½ oz gin
  • ½ oz Green Chartreuse
  • ½ oz Yellow Chartreuse

Steps

  1. Fill the mixing glass with ice.
  2. Add 1½ oz gin to the mixing glass.
  3. Add ½ oz Green Chartreuse to the mixing glass.
  4. Add ½ oz Yellow Chartreuse to the mixing glass.
  5. Stir with a bar spoon for about 30 seconds, until well chilled.
  6. Strain into a chilled coupe.
