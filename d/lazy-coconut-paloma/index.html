Lazy Coconut Paloma

Coconut rum and fresh grapefruit juice over ice, lengthened with soda. The lazy part is skipping the tequila: a soft, tropical, bittersweet long drink.

Ingredients

  • 1½ oz coconut rum
  • 3 oz fresh grapefruit juice
  • 2 oz soda water

Steps

  1. Add ice cubes to a highball glass.
  2. Add 1½ oz coconut rum to the highball glass.
  3. Add 3 oz fresh grapefruit juice to the highball glass.
  4. Top with 2 oz soda water.
  5. Give it a gentle stir.
