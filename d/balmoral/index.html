STIRRED · COUPE

Balmoral

Scotch with a little sweet and dry vermouth and a couple of dashes of Angostura. A perfect Rob Roy that keeps the whisky in charge, named for the royal estate.

Ingredients

  • 1½ oz blended Scotch
  • ½ oz sweet vermouth
  • ½ oz dry vermouth
  • 2 dashes Angostura bitters

Steps

  1. Fill the mixing glass with ice.
  2. Add 1½ oz blended Scotch to the mixing glass.
  3. Add ½ oz sweet vermouth to the mixing glass.
  4. Add ½ oz dry vermouth to the mixing glass.
  5. Add 2 dashes Angostura bitters to the mixing glass.
  6. Stir with a bar spoon for about 30 seconds, until well chilled.
  7. Strain into a chilled coupe.
  8. Express a lemon peel over the drink and drop it in.
