BUILT · HIGHBALL

AT&T

Vodka and gin share one tall glass of tonic over ice, with a wedge of lime. A crisp, bitter highball for anyone who can't choose between the two.

Ingredients

  • 1 oz vodka
  • 1 oz gin
  • 4 oz tonic water

Steps

  1. Add ice cubes to a highball glass.
  2. Add 1 oz vodka to the highball glass.
  3. Add 1 oz gin to the highball glass.
  4. Top with 4 oz tonic water.
  5. Give it a gentle stir.
  6. Garnish with a lime wedge on the rim.
