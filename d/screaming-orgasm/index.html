SHAKEN · COUPE

Screaming Orgasm

Vodka, Irish cream and coffee liqueur shaken cold and served up. Rich, sweet and creamy, like an iced coffee with a kick.

Ingredients

  • 1 oz vodka
  • 1½ oz Irish cream
  • ½ oz coffee liqueur

Steps

  1. Fill the shaker with ice.
  2. Add 1 oz vodka to the shaker.
  3. Add 1½ oz Irish cream to the shaker.
  4. Add ½ oz coffee liqueur to the shaker.
  5. Cap the shaker and shake hard for about 12 seconds.
  6. Strain into a chilled coupe.
