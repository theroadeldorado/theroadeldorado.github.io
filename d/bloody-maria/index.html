Bloody Maria

The Bloody Mary moved south of the border: tequila in place of vodka, with lemon, hot sauce, Worcestershire and a little port for depth. Earthy, peppery and built for a late breakfast.

Ingredients

  • 2 oz blanco tequila
  • 4 oz tomato juice
  • ½ oz fresh lemon juice
  • 2 dashes hot sauce
  • 4 dashes Worcestershire sauce
  • ½ oz ruby port, tawny if you have it

Steps

  1. Wet the rim of a highball glass and roll it in salt.
  2. Add ice cubes to the highball glass.
  3. Add 2 oz blanco tequila to the highball glass.
  4. Add 4 oz tomato juice to the highball glass.
  5. Add ½ oz fresh lemon juice to the highball glass.
  6. Add 2 dashes hot sauce to the highball glass.
  7. Add 4 dashes Worcestershire sauce to the highball glass.
  8. Add ½ oz ruby port (tawny if you have it) to the highball glass.
  9. Give it a gentle stir.
  10. Garnish with a lemon wheel and a celery stalk.
