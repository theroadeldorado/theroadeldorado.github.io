SHAKEN · COUPE

Orgasm

Crème de cacao, amaretto, orange liqueur and vodka shaken with cream and served up. A cheeky dessert drink: chocolate, almond and orange in a silky glass.

Ingredients

  • ½ oz crème de cacao
  • ½ oz amaretto
  • ½ oz Cointreau
  • ½ oz vodka
  • 1 oz heavy cream

Steps

  1. Fill the shaker with ice.
  2. Add ½ oz crème de cacao to the shaker.
  3. Add ½ oz amaretto to the shaker.
  4. Add ½ oz Cointreau to the shaker.
  5. Add ½ oz vodka to the shaker.
  6. Add 1 oz heavy cream to the shaker.
  7. Cap the shaker and shake hard for about 12 seconds.
  8. Strain into a chilled coupe.
