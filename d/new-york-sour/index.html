SHAKEN · ROCKS

New York Sour

A rye Whiskey Sour over ice with a float of dry claret resting on top. The wine adds tannin and dark fruit to every sip; the IBA's take on a nineteenth-century bar trick.

Ingredients

  • 2 oz rye whiskey
  • ¾ oz simple syrup
  • 1 oz fresh lemon juice
  • ¼ oz egg white
  • ½ oz red wine, floated on top

Steps

  1. Fill the shaker with ice. Add ice cubes to a rocks glass.
  2. Add 2 oz rye whiskey to the shaker.
  3. Add ¾ oz simple syrup to the shaker.
  4. Add 1 oz fresh lemon juice to the shaker.
  5. Add ¼ oz egg white to the shaker.
  6. Cap the shaker and shake hard for about 12 seconds.
  7. Strain into the rocks glass.
  8. Over the back of a spoon, gently pour ½ oz red wine onto the drink so it rests on top.
  9. Express a lemon peel over the drink and drop it in.
