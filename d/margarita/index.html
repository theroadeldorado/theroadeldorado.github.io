SHAKEN · ROCKS

Margarita

Tequila, orange liqueur and lime over ice, with a salted rim.

Ingredients

  • 2 oz blanco tequila
  • 1 oz Cointreau
  • 1 oz fresh lime juice

Steps

  1. Wet the rim of a rocks glass and roll it in salt.
  2. Fill the shaker with ice. Add ice cubes to the rocks glass.
  3. Add 2 oz blanco tequila to the shaker.
  4. Add 1 oz Cointreau to the shaker.
  5. Add 1 oz fresh lime juice to the shaker.
  6. Cap the shaker and shake hard for about 12 seconds.
  7. Strain into the rocks glass.
  8. Garnish with a lime wheel on the rim.
