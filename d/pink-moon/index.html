SHAKEN · ROCKS

Pink Moon

Gin, coconut rum and elderflower liqueur shaken with fresh lime and poured over ice. Floral and beachy at once: a gin sour with a soft coconut finish.

Ingredients

  • 1¼ oz gin
  • 1 oz coconut rum
  • ¾ oz elderflower liqueur
  • ¾ oz fresh lime juice

Steps

  1. Fill the shaker with ice. Add ice cubes to a rocks glass.
  2. Add 1¼ oz gin to the shaker.
  3. Add 1 oz coconut rum to the shaker.
  4. Add ¾ oz elderflower liqueur to the shaker.
  5. Add ¾ oz fresh lime juice to the shaker.
  6. Cap the shaker and shake hard for about 12 seconds.
  7. Strain into the rocks glass.
  8. Drop in a fresh raspberry.
