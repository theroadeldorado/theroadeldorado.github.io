Gin Rickey

Fresh lime and gin over ice, topped with soda and no sugar at all. The bone-dry Washington highball, as the Savoy printed it in 1930.

Ingredients

  • ½ oz fresh lime juice
  • 2 oz gin
  • Soda water, to top

Steps

  1. Add ice cubes to a highball glass.
  2. Add ½ oz fresh lime juice to the highball glass.
  3. Add 2 oz gin to the highball glass.
  4. Top with soda water.
  5. Give it a gentle stir.
  6. Garnish with a lime wedge on the rim.
