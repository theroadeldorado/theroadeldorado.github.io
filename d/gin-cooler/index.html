Gin Cooler

A tall gin and soda with a touch of sugar and a long spiral of orange peel hung over the rim. Old Mr. Boston's simplest summer cooler.

Ingredients

  • ¼ oz simple syrup
  • 2 oz gin
  • Soda water, to top

Steps

  1. Add ice cubes to a highball glass.
  2. Add ¼ oz simple syrup to the highball glass.
  3. Add 2 oz gin to the highball glass.
  4. Top with soda water.
  5. Give it a gentle stir.
  6. Express an orange peel over the drink and drop it in.
