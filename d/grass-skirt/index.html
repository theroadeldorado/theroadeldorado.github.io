SHAKEN · ROCKS

Grass Skirt

Gin shaken with Cointreau, pineapple juice and a touch of grenadine, then poured over ice. Fruity and easygoing, with a gin backbone under the tropical sweetness.

Ingredients

  • 1½ oz gin
  • ¾ oz Cointreau
  • 1 oz pineapple juice
  • ¼ oz pomegranate grenadine

Steps

  1. Fill the shaker with ice. Add ice cubes to a rocks glass.
  2. Add 1½ oz gin to the shaker.
  3. Add ¾ oz Cointreau to the shaker.
  4. Add 1 oz pineapple juice to the shaker.
  5. Add ¼ oz pomegranate grenadine to the shaker.
  6. Cap the shaker and shake hard for about 12 seconds.
  7. Strain into the rocks glass.
  8. Garnish with a pineapple wedge on the rim.
