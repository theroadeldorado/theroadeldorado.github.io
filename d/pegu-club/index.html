Pegu Club

Gin, orange curaçao and fresh lime with a dash each of aromatic and orange bitters, shaken and served up. The house drink of a colonial club in Rangoon: dry, zesty and quietly spiced.

Ingredients

  • 2 oz gin
  • ¾ oz orange curaçao
  • ¾ oz fresh lime juice
  • 1 dash Angostura bitters
  • 1 dash orange bitters

Steps

  1. Fill the shaker with ice.
  2. Add 2 oz gin to the shaker.
  3. Add ¾ oz orange curaçao to the shaker.
  4. Add ¾ oz fresh lime juice to the shaker.
  5. Add 1 dash Angostura bitters to the shaker.
  6. Add 1 dash orange bitters to the shaker.
  7. Cap the shaker and shake hard for about 12 seconds.
  8. Strain into a chilled coupe.
  9. Garnish with a lime wheel on the rim.
