SHAKEN · ROCKS

Blue Mountain

Jamaican rum and coffee liqueur shaken with vodka, orange and lime. Named for the island's coffee-growing peaks, it tastes of roast and citrus, as Time-Life's 1970 Caribbean cookbook printed it.

Ingredients

  • 1½ oz aged rum
  • ¾ oz vodka
  • ¾ oz coffee liqueur
  • 1 oz fresh orange juice
  • ½ oz fresh lime juice

Steps

  1. Fill the shaker with ice. Add ice cubes to a rocks glass.
  2. Add 1½ oz aged rum to the shaker.
  3. Add ¾ oz vodka to the shaker.
  4. Add ¾ oz coffee liqueur to the shaker.
  5. Add 1 oz fresh orange juice to the shaker.
  6. Add ½ oz fresh lime juice to the shaker.
  7. Cap the shaker and shake hard for about 12 seconds.
  8. Strain into the rocks glass.
  9. Garnish with an orange slice and coffee beans.
