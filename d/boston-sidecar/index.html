Boston Sidecar

Old Mr. Boston's Sidecar: brandy and light rum in equal measure with orange liqueur and lime. Lighter on its feet than the original, with a sharper, tropical edge.

Ingredients

  • ¾ oz cognac
  • ¾ oz white rum
  • ¾ oz Cointreau
  • ½ oz fresh lime juice

Steps

  1. Fill the shaker with ice.
  2. Add ¾ oz cognac to the shaker.
  3. Add ¾ oz white rum to the shaker.
  4. Add ¾ oz Cointreau to the shaker.
  5. Add ½ oz fresh lime juice to the shaker.
  6. Cap the shaker and shake hard for about 12 seconds.
  7. Strain into a chilled coupe.
  8. Express a lemon peel over the drink and drop it in.
