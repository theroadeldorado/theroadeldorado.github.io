SHAKEN · ROCKS

Acapulco

A rum sour with a little orange liqueur, shaken with egg white until silky and served over ice with mint. Fresh and resort-easy.

Ingredients

  • 2 oz white rum
  • ¾ oz fresh lime juice
  • ½ oz simple syrup
  • ¼ oz Cointreau
  • ¾ oz egg white

Steps

  1. Fill the shaker with ice. Add ice cubes to a rocks glass.
  2. Add 2 oz white rum to the shaker.
  3. Add ¾ oz fresh lime juice to the shaker.
  4. Add ½ oz simple syrup to the shaker.
  5. Add ¼ oz Cointreau to the shaker.
  6. Add ¾ oz egg white to the shaker.
  7. Cap the shaker and shake hard for about 12 seconds.
  8. Strain into the rocks glass.
  9. Garnish with a sprig of mint.
