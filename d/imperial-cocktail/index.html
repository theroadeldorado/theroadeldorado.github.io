SHAKEN · COUPE

Imperial Cocktail

Gin and Aperol in equal measure, shaken with fresh lime and served up. Bittersweet orange and juniper with a tart edge: an aperitivo with a sour's snap.

Ingredients

  • ½ oz fresh lime juice
  • 1¼ oz gin
  • 1¼ oz Aperol

Steps

  1. Fill the shaker with ice.
  2. Add ½ oz fresh lime juice to the shaker.
  3. Add 1¼ oz gin to the shaker.
  4. Add 1¼ oz Aperol to the shaker.
  5. Cap the shaker and shake hard for about 12 seconds.
  6. Strain into a chilled coupe.
