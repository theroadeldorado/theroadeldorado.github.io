BUILT · HIGHBALL

50/50

Vodka, a touch of vanilla and Grand Marnier over fresh orange juice. It tastes like the orange-and-vanilla ice pop off a summer ice-cream truck.

Ingredients

  • 1½ oz vodka
  • ¼ oz vanilla syrup
  • ½ oz Grand Marnier
  • 4 oz fresh orange juice

Steps

  1. Add ice cubes to a highball glass.
  2. Add 1½ oz vodka to the highball glass.
  3. Add ¼ oz vanilla syrup to the highball glass.
  4. Add ½ oz Grand Marnier to the highball glass.
  5. Add 4 oz fresh orange juice to the highball glass.
  6. Give it a gentle stir.
  7. Garnish with an orange wheel on the rim.
