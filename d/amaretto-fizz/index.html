Amaretto Fizz

Dry white wine over ice with fresh orange juice and a measure of amaretto. A light, nutty wine cooler for warm evenings.

Ingredients

  • 1¼ oz amaretto
  • 2 oz fresh orange juice
  • 5 oz white wine

Steps

  1. Add ice cubes to a highball glass.
  2. Add 1¼ oz amaretto to the highball glass.
  3. Add 2 oz fresh orange juice to the highball glass.
  4. Add 5 oz white wine to the highball glass.
  5. Give it a gentle stir.
  6. Express an orange peel over the drink and drop it in.
