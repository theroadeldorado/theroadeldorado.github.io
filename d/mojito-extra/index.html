Mojito Extra

A darker Mojito: mint crushed with lemon and sugar, then Jamaican-style dark rum, crushed ice, soda and a few dashes of Angostura. Deeper and spicier than the Havana original.

Ingredients

  • 8 mint leaves
  • ¾ oz fresh lemon juice
  • ¾ oz simple syrup
  • 2 oz dark rum
  • 2 dashes Angostura bitters
  • Soda water, to top

Steps

  1. Muddle 8 mint leaves in a highball glass.
  2. Add crushed ice to the highball glass.
  3. Add ¾ oz fresh lemon juice to the highball glass.
  4. Add ¾ oz simple syrup to the highball glass.
  5. Add 2 oz dark rum to the highball glass.
  6. Add 2 dashes Angostura bitters to the highball glass.
  7. Top with soda water.
  8. Give it a gentle stir.
  9. Garnish with a sprig of mint. Add a straw.
