Sol y Sombra

"Sun and shade": brandy and anise spirit in equal parts, served neat. A Spanish bar-room warmer, named for the cheaper seats at the bullring, that tastes of grape and licorice.

Ingredients

  • 1½ oz cognac
  • 1½ oz pastis

Steps

  1. Add 1½ oz cognac to a rocks glass.
  2. Add 1½ oz pastis to the rocks glass.
  3. Give it a gentle stir.
