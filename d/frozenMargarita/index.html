Frozen Margarita

The Margarita blended with ice into a slush.

Ingredients

  • 2 oz blanco tequila
  • 1 oz Cointreau
  • 1 oz fresh lime juice

Steps

  1. Wet the rim of a rocks glass and roll it in salt.
  2. Blend 2 oz blanco tequila, 1 oz Cointreau and 1 oz fresh lime juice with ½ cup of ice until smooth, then pour into the rocks glass.
  3. Garnish with a lime wedge on the rim.
