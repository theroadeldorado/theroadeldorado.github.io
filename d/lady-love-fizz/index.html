Lady Love Fizz

Gin shaken hard with lemon, sugar, egg white and a splash of cream, then topped with soda over ice. An Old Mr. Boston fizz that drinks like a lighter, quicker Ramos: silky, tart and soft.

Ingredients

  • ½ oz simple syrup
  • ¾ oz fresh lemon juice
  • ¾ oz egg white
  • 2 oz gin
  • ¼ oz heavy cream
  • Soda water, to top

Steps

  1. Fill the shaker with ice. Add ice cubes to a highball glass.
  2. Add ½ oz simple syrup to the shaker.
  3. Add ¾ oz fresh lemon juice to the shaker.
  4. Add ¾ oz egg white to the shaker.
  5. Add 2 oz gin to the shaker.
  6. Add ¼ oz heavy cream to the shaker.
  7. Cap the shaker and shake hard for about 12 seconds.
  8. Strain into the highball glass.
  9. Top with soda water.
