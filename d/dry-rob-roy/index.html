Dry Rob Roy

The Rob Roy made with dry vermouth in place of sweet, stirred cold and finished with lemon peel. Leaner and crisper, letting the Scotch do the talking.

Ingredients

  • 2½ oz blended Scotch
  • ½ oz dry vermouth

Steps

  1. Fill the mixing glass with ice.
  2. Add 2½ oz blended Scotch to the mixing glass.
  3. Add ½ oz dry vermouth to the mixing glass.
  4. Stir with a bar spoon for about 30 seconds, until well chilled.
  5. Strain into a chilled Nick & Nora glass.
  6. Express a lemon peel over the drink and drop it in.
