Death in the Afternoon

Hemingway's contribution to a 1935 book of writers' cocktails: a jigger of absinthe with iced Champagne poured over until it turns cloudy. Anise-dry and dangerous.

Ingredients

  • 1½ oz absinthe
  • Champagne, to top

Steps

  1. Add 1½ oz absinthe to a flute.
  2. Top with Champagne.
  3. Give it a gentle stir.
