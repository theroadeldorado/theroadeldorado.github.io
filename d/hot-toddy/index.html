Hot Toddy

Bourbon, honey and lemon in a mug of hot tea, with a cinnamon stick to stir it. The old cold-weather remedy: soothing, gently sweet and warming right through.

Ingredients

  • ¾ oz honey syrup
  • ½ oz fresh lemon juice
  • 2 oz bourbon
  • 4 oz black tea, freshly brewed and hot, or plain hot water

Steps

  1. Add ¾ oz honey syrup to an Irish coffee glass.
  2. Add ½ oz fresh lemon juice to the Irish coffee glass.
  3. Add 2 oz bourbon to the Irish coffee glass.
  4. Add 4 oz black tea (freshly brewed and hot, or plain hot water) to the Irish coffee glass.
  5. Give it a gentle stir.
  6. Garnish with a cinnamon stick and a lemon wheel.
