Black Russian

Vodka and coffee liqueur over ice: the White Russian before the cream.

Ingredients

  • 2 oz vodka
  • ¾ oz coffee liqueur

Steps

  1. Add ice cubes to a rocks glass.
  2. Add 2 oz vodka to the rocks glass.
  3. Add ¾ oz coffee liqueur to the rocks glass.
  4. Give it a gentle stir.
  5. Add a stirrer.
