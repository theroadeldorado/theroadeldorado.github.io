STIRRED · COUPE

Stinger

Cognac with a cool edge of crème de menthe. An after-dinner classic.

Ingredients

  • 1¾ oz cognac
  • ¾ oz white crème de menthe

Steps

  1. Fill the mixing glass with ice.
  2. Add 1¾ oz cognac to the mixing glass.
  3. Add ¾ oz white crème de menthe to the mixing glass.
  4. Stir with a bar spoon for about 30 seconds, until well chilled.
  5. Strain into a chilled coupe.
  6. Garnish with a sprig of mint.
