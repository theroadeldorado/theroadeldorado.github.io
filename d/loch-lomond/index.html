STIRRED · COUPE

Loch Lomond

Scotch stirred with a half measure each of Drambuie and dry vermouth, then finished with lemon peel. Honeyed heather from the liqueur, a dry, winey lift from the vermouth: a Rusty Nail with better manners.

Ingredients

  • 2 oz blended Scotch
  • ½ oz Drambuie
  • ½ oz dry vermouth

Steps

  1. Fill the mixing glass with ice.
  2. Add 2 oz blended Scotch to the mixing glass.
  3. Add ½ oz Drambuie to the mixing glass.
  4. Add ½ oz dry vermouth to the mixing glass.
  5. Stir with a bar spoon for about 30 seconds, until well chilled.
  6. Strain into a chilled coupe.
  7. Express a lemon peel over the drink and drop it in.
