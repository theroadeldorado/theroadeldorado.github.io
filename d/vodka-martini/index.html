Vodka Martini

Ice-cold vodka stirred with a little dry vermouth and served with olives. Cleaner than the gin original, with no botanicals to hide behind: just a silky, bracing chill and a savory finish.

Ingredients

  • 2¼ oz vodka
  • ½ oz dry vermouth

Steps

  1. Fill the mixing glass with ice.
  2. Add 2¼ oz vodka to the mixing glass.
  3. Add ½ oz dry vermouth to the mixing glass.
  4. Stir with a bar spoon for about 30 seconds, until well chilled.
  5. Strain into a chilled martini glass.
  6. Garnish with olives on a pick.
