Tequila Fizz

Tequila shaken with lemon, pomegranate grenadine and egg white, then lengthened with ginger ale over ice. A soft, foamy fizz from a 1970s book of tequila drinks: fruity and gently spiced, with the agave showing through.

Ingredients

  • 1½ oz blanco tequila
  • ¾ oz fresh lemon juice
  • ¾ oz pomegranate grenadine
  • ¾ oz egg white
  • 3 oz ginger ale

Steps

  1. Fill the shaker with ice. Add ice cubes to a highball glass.
  2. Add 1½ oz blanco tequila to the shaker.
  3. Add ¾ oz fresh lemon juice to the shaker.
  4. Add ¾ oz pomegranate grenadine to the shaker.
  5. Add ¾ oz egg white to the shaker.
  6. Cap the shaker and shake hard for about 12 seconds.
  7. Strain into the highball glass.
  8. Top with 3 oz ginger ale.
  9. Garnish with a lime wedge on the rim.
