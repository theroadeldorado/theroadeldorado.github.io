BLENDED · HIGHBALL

Adam Sunrise

Vodka blended with fresh lemon juice and sugar into a frozen lemonade. Sweet-tart, icy and made for the porch.

Ingredients

  • 2 oz vodka
  • 1¼ oz fresh lemon juice
  • 1 oz simple syrup

Steps

  1. Blend 2 oz vodka, 1¼ oz fresh lemon juice and 1 oz simple syrup with a cup of ice until smooth, then pour into a highball glass.
  2. Garnish with a lemon wheel on the rim. Add a straw.
