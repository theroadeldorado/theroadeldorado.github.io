Almond Chocolate Coffee

Hot coffee spiked with amaretto and chocolate liqueur. A warming mug that tastes of marzipan and cocoa.

Ingredients

  • ¾ oz amaretto
  • ½ oz crème de cacao
  • 6 oz hot coffee

Steps

  1. Add ¾ oz amaretto to an Irish coffee glass.
  2. Add ½ oz crème de cacao to the Irish coffee glass.
  3. Add 6 oz hot coffee to the Irish coffee glass.
  4. Give it a gentle stir.
