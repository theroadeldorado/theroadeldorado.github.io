Radioactive Long Island Iced Tea

Four clear spirits and orange liqueur shaken with raspberry, melon and coconut, fresh lemon and a little sugar, then lengthened with soda over ice. A fruit-loaded Long Island with real kick.

Ingredients

  • ½ oz white rum
  • ½ oz vodka
  • ½ oz blanco tequila
  • ½ oz gin
  • ½ oz Cointreau
  • ¼ oz raspberry liqueur
  • ¼ oz melon liqueur
  • ¼ oz coconut rum
  • ¾ oz fresh lemon juice
  • ¼ oz simple syrup
  • 2 oz soda water

Steps

  1. Fill the shaker with ice. Add ice cubes to a highball glass.
  2. Add ½ oz white rum to the shaker.
  3. Add ½ oz vodka to the shaker.
  4. Add ½ oz blanco tequila to the shaker.
  5. Add ½ oz gin to the shaker.
  6. Add ½ oz Cointreau to the shaker.
  7. Add ¼ oz raspberry liqueur to the shaker.
  8. Add ¼ oz melon liqueur to the shaker.
  9. Add ¼ oz coconut rum to the shaker.
  10. Add ¾ oz fresh lemon juice to the shaker.
  11. Add ¼ oz simple syrup to the shaker.
  12. Cap the shaker and shake hard for about 12 seconds.
  13. Strain into the highball glass.
  14. Top with 2 oz soda water.
  15. Garnish with a lemon wheel on the rim. Add a straw.
