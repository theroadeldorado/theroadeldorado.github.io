BUILT · ROCKS

Old Fashioned

Whiskey, sugar and bitters over a big cube. The original cocktail.

Ingredients

  • ¼ oz simple syrup
  • 2 dashes Angostura bitters
  • 1 dash orange bitters (optional)
  • 2 oz bourbon

Steps

  1. Add a big ice cube to a rocks glass.
  2. Add ¼ oz simple syrup to the rocks glass.
  3. Add 2 dashes Angostura bitters to the rocks glass.
  4. Add 1 dash orange bitters (optional) to the rocks glass.
  5. Add 2 oz bourbon to the rocks glass.
  6. Give it a gentle stir.
  7. Garnish with an orange peel and a cherry.
