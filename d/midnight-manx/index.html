SHAKEN · HIGHBALL

Midnight Manx

Coffee liqueur, Irish cream, cream and a shot of espresso shaken and poured long over ice. An iced coffee for after dinner: sweet, creamy and properly roasty.

Ingredients

  • 1 oz coffee liqueur
  • 1 oz Irish cream
  • 1½ oz heavy cream
  • 1 oz espresso

Steps

  1. Fill the shaker with ice. Add ice cubes to a highball glass.
  2. Add 1 oz coffee liqueur to the shaker.
  3. Add 1 oz Irish cream to the shaker.
  4. Add 1½ oz heavy cream to the shaker.
  5. Add 1 oz espresso to the shaker.
  6. Cap the shaker and shake hard for about 12 seconds.
  7. Strain into the highball glass.
