After Dinner Mint

Hot chocolate spiked with Southern Comfort, vodka and a little crème de menthe. A warming mug that tastes like the chocolate mint left on your pillow.

Ingredients

  • ¾ oz Southern Comfort
  • ½ oz white crème de menthe
  • ½ oz vodka
  • 4 oz hot chocolate

Steps

  1. Add ¾ oz Southern Comfort to an Irish coffee glass.
  2. Add ½ oz white crème de menthe to the Irish coffee glass.
  3. Add ½ oz vodka to the Irish coffee glass.
  4. Add 4 oz hot chocolate to the Irish coffee glass.
  5. Give it a gentle stir.
