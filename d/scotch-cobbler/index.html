Scotch Cobbler

Scotch over crushed ice with a splash each of brandy and orange curaçao, crowned with mint. A short, frosty cobbler where the whisky does the talking.

Ingredients

  • 2 oz blended Scotch
  • ¼ oz cognac
  • ¼ oz orange curaçao

Steps

  1. Add crushed ice to a rocks glass.
  2. Add 2 oz blended Scotch to the rocks glass.
  3. Add ¼ oz cognac to the rocks glass.
  4. Add ¼ oz orange curaçao to the rocks glass.
  5. Give it a gentle stir.
  6. Garnish with a sprig of mint. Add a straw.
