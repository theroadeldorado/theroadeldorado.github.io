SHAKEN · ROCKS

Surf City Lifesaver

Gin shaken with anise spirit, Irish cream and a splash of Grand Marnier and poured over ice. An unlikely beach-bar mix that works: licorice and juniper first, softened by the cream, with orange on the finish.

Ingredients

  • ½ oz pastis
  • ½ oz Irish cream
  • 1 oz gin
  • ¼ oz Grand Marnier

Steps

  1. Fill the shaker with ice. Add ice cubes to a rocks glass.
  2. Add ½ oz pastis to the shaker.
  3. Add ½ oz Irish cream to the shaker.
  4. Add 1 oz gin to the shaker.
  5. Add ¼ oz Grand Marnier to the shaker.
  6. Cap the shaker and shake hard for about 12 seconds.
  7. Strain into the rocks glass.
