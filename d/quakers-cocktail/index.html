SHAKEN · COUPE

Quaker's Cocktail

Brandy and rum in equal measure, shaken with fresh lemon and raspberry syrup. A Savoy sour that's richer than a daiquiri and brighter than a sidecar, with a berry finish.

Ingredients

  • 1 oz cognac
  • 1 oz white rum
  • ½ oz fresh lemon juice
  • ½ oz raspberry syrup

Steps

  1. Fill the shaker with ice.
  2. Add 1 oz cognac to the shaker.
  3. Add 1 oz white rum to the shaker.
  4. Add ½ oz fresh lemon juice to the shaker.
  5. Add ½ oz raspberry syrup to the shaker.
  6. Cap the shaker and shake hard for about 12 seconds.
  7. Strain into a chilled coupe.
