SHAKEN · ROCKS

Grand Blue

Coconut rum, peach schnapps and curaçao shaken with fresh lemon and served over ice. Sweet, tropical and tangy: a beach-bar drink made with real citrus.

Ingredients

  • ¾ oz coconut rum
  • ¾ oz peach schnapps
  • ¾ oz blue curaçao
  • 1 oz fresh lemon juice
  • ¼ oz simple syrup

Steps

  1. Fill the shaker with ice. Add ice cubes to a rocks glass.
  2. Add ¾ oz coconut rum to the shaker.
  3. Add ¾ oz peach schnapps to the shaker.
  4. Add ¾ oz blue curaçao to the shaker.
  5. Add 1 oz fresh lemon juice to the shaker.
  6. Add ¼ oz simple syrup to the shaker.
  7. Cap the shaker and shake hard for about 12 seconds.
  8. Strain into the rocks glass.
