SHAKEN · COUPE

Scooter

Brandy, amaretto and cream in equal parts, shaken cold and finished with nutmeg. A 1970s after-dinner drink that tastes like almond custard with a warm brandy finish.

Ingredients

  • 1 oz cognac
  • 1 oz amaretto
  • 1 oz heavy cream

Steps

  1. Fill the shaker with ice.
  2. Add 1 oz cognac to the shaker.
  3. Add 1 oz amaretto to the shaker.
  4. Add 1 oz heavy cream to the shaker.
  5. Cap the shaker and shake hard for about 12 seconds.
  6. Strain into a chilled coupe.
  7. Grate a little nutmeg over the top.
