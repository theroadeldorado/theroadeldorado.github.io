SHAKEN · COUPE

White Lady

Gin, orange liqueur and lemon, shaken hard: crisp, dry and elegant.

Ingredients

  • 1¼ oz gin
  • 1 oz Cointreau
  • ¾ oz fresh lemon juice

Steps

  1. Fill the shaker with ice.
  2. Add 1¼ oz gin to the shaker.
  3. Add 1 oz Cointreau to the shaker.
  4. Add ¾ oz fresh lemon juice to the shaker.
  5. Cap the shaker and shake hard for about 12 seconds.
  6. Strain into a chilled coupe.
  7. Express a lemon peel over the drink and drop it in.
