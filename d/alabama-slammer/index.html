SHAKEN · HIGHBALL

Alabama Slammer

Southern Comfort, amaretto and sloe gin in equal parts, shaken with fresh orange juice. A college-bar favorite: fruity, nutty and sweeter than it is strong.

Ingredients

  • 1 oz Southern Comfort
  • 1 oz amaretto
  • 1 oz sloe gin
  • 2 oz fresh orange juice

Steps

  1. Fill the shaker with ice. Add ice cubes to a highball glass.
  2. Add 1 oz Southern Comfort to the shaker.
  3. Add 1 oz amaretto to the shaker.
  4. Add 1 oz sloe gin to the shaker.
  5. Add 2 oz fresh orange juice to the shaker.
  6. Cap the shaker and shake hard for about 12 seconds.
  7. Strain into the highball glass.
  8. Garnish with an orange wheel and a cherry.
