New York Lemonade

Citrus vodka and Grand Marnier shaken with fresh lemon and sugar, served up with a splash of soda. A grown-up lemonade: sharp, orange-scented and lightly fizzy.

Ingredients

  • 2 oz citrus vodka
  • ¾ oz Grand Marnier
  • 1 oz fresh lemon juice
  • ½ oz simple syrup
  • 1 oz soda water

Steps

  1. Fill the shaker with ice.
  2. Add 2 oz citrus vodka to the shaker.
  3. Add ¾ oz Grand Marnier to the shaker.
  4. Add 1 oz fresh lemon juice to the shaker.
  5. Add ½ oz simple syrup to the shaker.
  6. Cap the shaker and shake hard for about 12 seconds.
  7. Strain into a chilled coupe.
  8. Top with 1 oz soda water.
  9. Express a lemon peel over the drink and drop it in.
