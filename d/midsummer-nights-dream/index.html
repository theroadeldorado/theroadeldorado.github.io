SHAKEN · HIGHBALL

Midsummer Night's Dream

Fresh strawberries crushed with vodka, kirsch and strawberry liqueur, shaken and topped with tonic. Fruity up front, with a dry, bitter finish.

Ingredients

  • 4 strawberries
  • 1½ oz vodka
  • ¾ oz kirsch
  • ¼ oz strawberry liqueur
  • Tonic water, to top

Steps

  1. Muddle 4 strawberries in the shaker.
  2. Fill the shaker with ice. Add ice cubes to a highball glass.
  3. Add 1½ oz vodka to the shaker.
  4. Add ¾ oz kirsch to the shaker.
  5. Add ¼ oz strawberry liqueur to the shaker.
  6. Cap the shaker and shake hard for about 12 seconds.
  7. Strain into the highball glass.
  8. Top with tonic water.
  9. Garnish with a strawberry on the rim.
