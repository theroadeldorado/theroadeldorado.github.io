STIRRED · COUPE

Highland Fling

Scotch stirred with sweet vermouth and orange bitters, then finished with an olive. A drier cousin of the Rob Roy from Burke's 1936 guide; the olive's brine plays nicely against the malt.

Ingredients

  • 2¼ oz blended Scotch
  • ¾ oz sweet vermouth
  • 2 dashes orange bitters

Steps

  1. Fill the mixing glass with ice.
  2. Add 2¼ oz blended Scotch to the mixing glass.
  3. Add ¾ oz sweet vermouth to the mixing glass.
  4. Add 2 dashes orange bitters to the mixing glass.
  5. Stir with a bar spoon for about 30 seconds, until well chilled.
  6. Strain into a chilled coupe.
  7. Garnish with olives on a pick.
