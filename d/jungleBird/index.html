SHAKEN · ROCKS

Jungle Bird

Dark rum, Campari and pineapple: tiki with a bitter edge.

Ingredients

  • 1½ oz dark rum
  • ¾ oz Campari
  • 1½ oz pineapple juice
  • ½ oz fresh lime juice
  • ½ oz simple syrup

Steps

  1. Fill the shaker with ice. Add ice cubes to a rocks glass.
  2. Add 1½ oz dark rum to the shaker.
  3. Add ¾ oz Campari to the shaker.
  4. Add 1½ oz pineapple juice to the shaker.
  5. Add ½ oz fresh lime juice to the shaker.
  6. Add ½ oz simple syrup to the shaker.
  7. Cap the shaker and shake hard for about 12 seconds.
  8. Strain into the rocks glass.
  9. Garnish with a pineapple wedge and a cherry.
