Blue Margarita

A Margarita blended with crushed ice, using blue curaçao for its orange liqueur. Slushy, tart and a little bitter at the edges from the curaçao's peel.

Ingredients

  • 2 oz blanco tequila
  • 1 oz blue curaçao
  • 1 oz fresh lime juice
  • ¾ oz simple syrup

Steps

  1. Wet the rim of a coupe and roll it in salt.
  2. Blend 2 oz blanco tequila, 1 oz blue curaçao, 1 oz fresh lime juice and ¾ oz simple syrup with ½ cup of ice until smooth, then pour into the coupe.
  3. Garnish with a lime wheel on the rim. Add a straw.
