Apricot Lady

Light rum, apricot liqueur, a touch of triple sec, lime and egg white, blended briefly with crushed ice. A soft, frothy apricot sour from Old Mr. Boston.

Ingredients

  • 1¼ oz white rum
  • 1 oz apricot brandy
  • ¼ oz Cointreau
  • ½ oz fresh lime juice
  • ¾ oz egg white

Steps

  1. Blend 1¼ oz white rum, 1 oz apricot brandy, ¼ oz Cointreau, ½ oz fresh lime juice and ¾ oz egg white with ½ cup of ice until smooth, then pour into a rocks glass.
  2. Garnish with an orange slice.
