SHAKEN · COUPE

After Supper Cocktail

Apricot liqueur and orange curaçao, half and half, with just a touch of lemon. A sweet little Savoy nightcap, meant for sipping slowly.

Ingredients

  • 1¼ oz apricot brandy
  • 1¼ oz orange curaçao
  • ¼ oz fresh lemon juice

Steps

  1. Fill the shaker with ice.
  2. Add 1¼ oz apricot brandy to the shaker.
  3. Add 1¼ oz orange curaçao to the shaker.
  4. Add ¼ oz fresh lemon juice to the shaker.
  5. Cap the shaker and shake hard for about 12 seconds.
  6. Strain into a chilled coupe.
  7. Express a lemon peel over the drink and drop it in.
