SHAKEN · COUPE

Army Special

Vodka and gin in equal measure, shaken with fresh lime and sugar. A Gimlet that's been given a second spirit: sharp, clean and stronger than it looks.

Ingredients

  • 1 oz vodka
  • 1 oz gin
  • ¾ oz fresh lime juice
  • ¾ oz simple syrup

Steps

  1. Fill the shaker with ice.
  2. Add 1 oz vodka to the shaker.
  3. Add 1 oz gin to the shaker.
  4. Add ¾ oz fresh lime juice to the shaker.
  5. Add ¾ oz simple syrup to the shaker.
  6. Cap the shaker and shake hard for about 12 seconds.
  7. Strain into a chilled coupe.
  8. Garnish with a lime wheel on the rim.
