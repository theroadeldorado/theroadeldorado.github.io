SHAKEN · COUPE

Golden Dream

Galliano, orange liqueur, fresh orange and cream. A citrusy dessert drink from the sixties.

Ingredients

  • ¾ oz Galliano
  • ¾ oz Cointreau
  • ¾ oz fresh orange juice
  • ½ oz heavy cream

Steps

  1. Fill the shaker with ice.
  2. Add ¾ oz Galliano to the shaker.
  3. Add ¾ oz Cointreau to the shaker.
  4. Add ¾ oz fresh orange juice to the shaker.
  5. Add ½ oz heavy cream to the shaker.
  6. Cap the shaker and shake hard for about 12 seconds.
  7. Strain into a chilled coupe.
