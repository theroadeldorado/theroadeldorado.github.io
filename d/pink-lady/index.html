SHAKEN · COUPE

Pink Lady

Gin and a splash of applejack shaken with lemon, real pomegranate grenadine and egg white. Named for a 1911 Broadway musical, it's a silky, tart-sweet sour with an orchard undertone.

Ingredients

  • 1½ oz gin
  • ½ oz applejack
  • ¾ oz fresh lemon juice
  • ¾ oz pomegranate grenadine
  • ¾ oz egg white

Steps

  1. Fill the shaker with ice.
  2. Add 1½ oz gin to the shaker.
  3. Add ½ oz applejack to the shaker.
  4. Add ¾ oz fresh lemon juice to the shaker.
  5. Add ¾ oz pomegranate grenadine to the shaker.
  6. Add ¾ oz egg white to the shaker.
  7. Cap the shaker and shake hard for about 12 seconds.
  8. Strain into a chilled coupe.
  9. Drop in a cocktail cherry.
