Lone Tree Cooler

Gin and a splash of dry vermouth over ice with a touch of sugar, lengthened with soda and hung with a long orange peel. A dry, breezy old-school cooler for a hot porch.

Ingredients

  • ¼ oz simple syrup
  • 2 oz gin
  • ½ oz dry vermouth
  • Soda water, to top

Steps

  1. Add ice cubes to a highball glass.
  2. Add ¼ oz simple syrup to the highball glass.
  3. Add 2 oz gin to the highball glass.
  4. Add ½ oz dry vermouth to the highball glass.
  5. Top with soda water.
  6. Give it a gentle stir.
  7. Express an orange peel over the drink and drop it in.
