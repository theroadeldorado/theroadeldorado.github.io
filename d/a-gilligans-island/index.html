SHAKEN · HIGHBALL

A Gilligan's Island

Vodka and peach schnapps shaken with fresh orange and cranberry juice. Long, fruity and easygoing, named for the castaway sitcom.

Ingredients

  • 1½ oz vodka
  • ¾ oz peach schnapps
  • 2½ oz fresh orange juice
  • 2½ oz cranberry juice

Steps

  1. Fill the shaker with ice. Add ice cubes to a highball glass.
  2. Add 1½ oz vodka to the shaker.
  3. Add ¾ oz peach schnapps to the shaker.
  4. Add 2½ oz fresh orange juice to the shaker.
  5. Add 2½ oz cranberry juice to the shaker.
  6. Cap the shaker and shake hard for about 12 seconds.
  7. Strain into the highball glass.
  8. Garnish with an orange wheel on the rim. Add a straw.
