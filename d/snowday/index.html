STIRRED · ROCKS

Snowday

Vodka, amaro and ruby port in equal parts, stirred with a splash of orange and a dash of bitters. Bittersweet and wintry, with plummy fruit and a hint of spice: a drink for watching the snow.

Ingredients

  • 1 oz vodka
  • 1 oz amaro
  • 1 oz ruby port
  • ¼ oz fresh orange juice
  • 1 dash Angostura bitters

Steps

  1. Fill the mixing glass with ice. Add a big ice cube to a rocks glass.
  2. Add 1 oz vodka to the mixing glass.
  3. Add 1 oz amaro to the mixing glass.
  4. Add 1 oz ruby port to the mixing glass.
  5. Add ¼ oz fresh orange juice to the mixing glass.
  6. Add 1 dash Angostura bitters to the mixing glass.
  7. Stir with a bar spoon for about 30 seconds, until well chilled.
  8. Strain into the rocks glass.
  9. Express an orange peel over the drink and drop it in.
