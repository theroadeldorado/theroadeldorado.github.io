BUILT · SHOT

B-52

A layered shot: coffee liqueur, then Irish cream, then Grand Marnier, each poured over a spoon.

Ingredients

  • ½ oz coffee liqueur
  • ½ oz Irish cream, layered
  • ½ oz Grand Marnier, layered on top

Steps

  1. Add ½ oz coffee liqueur to a shot glass.
  2. Hold a bar spoon upside down just above the coffee liqueur in the shot glass and slowly pour the Irish cream over its back so it settles on top.
  3. Pour the Grand Marnier over the spoon the same way, to make a third layer.
