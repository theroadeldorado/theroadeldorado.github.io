BUILT · HIGHBALL

Addison Special

Vodka and fresh orange juice over ice, sweetened with a spoonful of pomegranate grenadine. Easy, juicy and good at brunch.

Ingredients

  • 1½ oz vodka
  • ½ oz pomegranate grenadine
  • 4 oz fresh orange juice

Steps

  1. Add ice cubes to a highball glass.
  2. Add 1½ oz vodka to the highball glass.
  3. Add ½ oz pomegranate grenadine to the highball glass.
  4. Add 4 oz fresh orange juice to the highball glass.
  5. Give it a gentle stir.
  6. Garnish with an orange wheel on the rim.
