The Evil Blue Thing

Crème de cacao, blue curaçao and light rum stirred together over ice. A sweet after-dinner novelty that tastes of chocolate and orange peel, with the rum keeping it from turning into candy.

Ingredients

  • 1 oz crème de cacao
  • ¾ oz blue curaçao
  • 1 oz white rum

Steps

  1. Add ice cubes to a rocks glass.
  2. Add 1 oz crème de cacao to the rocks glass.
  3. Add ¾ oz blue curaçao to the rocks glass.
  4. Add 1 oz white rum to the rocks glass.
  5. Give it a gentle stir.
