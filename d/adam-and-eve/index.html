SHAKEN · COUPE

Adam & Eve

Gin and cognac in equal measure with blackcurrant liqueur and a squeeze of lemon. Fruity, rich and stronger than it tastes.

Ingredients

  • 1 oz gin
  • 1 oz cognac
  • ¾ oz crème de cassis
  • ¼ oz fresh lemon juice

Steps

  1. Fill the shaker with ice.
  2. Add 1 oz gin to the shaker.
  3. Add 1 oz cognac to the shaker.
  4. Add ¾ oz crème de cassis to the shaker.
  5. Add ¼ oz fresh lemon juice to the shaker.
  6. Cap the shaker and shake hard for about 12 seconds.
  7. Strain into a chilled coupe.
  8. Express a lemon peel over the drink and drop it in.
