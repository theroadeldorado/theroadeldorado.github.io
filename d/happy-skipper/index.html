Happy Skipper

Spiced rum over ice with a squeeze of fresh lime, lengthened with ginger ale. An easy sailor's highball that tastes of vanilla, warm spice and ginger.

Ingredients

  • 2 oz spiced rum
  • ½ oz fresh lime juice
  • 4 oz ginger ale

Steps

  1. Add ice cubes to a highball glass.
  2. Add 2 oz spiced rum to the highball glass.
  3. Add ½ oz fresh lime juice to the highball glass.
  4. Top with 4 oz ginger ale.
  5. Give it a gentle stir.
  6. Garnish with a lime wedge on the rim.
