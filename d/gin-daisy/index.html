SHAKEN · COUPE

Gin Daisy

Gin shaken with lemon, a little sugar and maraschino, then lifted with a splash of soda. Jerry Thomas's 1887 Daisy: tart, faintly cherried and sparkling.

Ingredients

  • ¼ oz simple syrup
  • ¼ oz maraschino liqueur
  • ¾ oz fresh lemon juice
  • 2 oz gin
  • Soda water, to top

Steps

  1. Fill the shaker with ice.
  2. Add ¼ oz simple syrup to the shaker.
  3. Add ¼ oz maraschino liqueur to the shaker.
  4. Add ¾ oz fresh lemon juice to the shaker.
  5. Add 2 oz gin to the shaker.
  6. Cap the shaker and shake hard for about 12 seconds.
  7. Strain into a chilled coupe.
  8. Top with soda water.
  9. Garnish with an orange slice.
