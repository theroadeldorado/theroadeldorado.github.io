SHAKEN · COUPE

Derby

Gin shaken with fresh mint and a couple of dashes of peach bitters, from the Savoy. Essentially ice-cold gin served neat, fresh with mint and a soft stone-fruit scent.

Ingredients

  • 2 dashes peach bitters
  • 8 mint leaves
  • 2 oz gin

Steps

  1. Muddle 8 mint leaves in the shaker.
  2. Fill the shaker with ice.
  3. Add 2 dashes peach bitters to the shaker.
  4. Add 2 oz gin to the shaker.
  5. Cap the shaker and shake hard for about 12 seconds.
  6. Strain into a chilled coupe.
  7. Garnish with a sprig of mint.
