SHAKEN · HIGHBALL

Shark Attack

Vodka shaken into fresh lemonade, with grenadine slipped in last so it trails down through the glass like a bite mark. A party drink that tastes like spiked lemonade.

Ingredients

  • 1½ oz vodka
  • 1 oz fresh lemon juice
  • ¾ oz simple syrup
  • ½ oz pomegranate grenadine, poured last so it sinks

Steps

  1. Fill the shaker with ice. Add ice cubes to a highball glass.
  2. Add 1½ oz vodka to the shaker.
  3. Add 1 oz fresh lemon juice to the shaker.
  4. Add ¾ oz simple syrup to the shaker.
  5. Cap the shaker and shake hard for about 12 seconds.
  6. Strain into the highball glass.
  7. Slowly pour ½ oz pomegranate grenadine down the inside of the highball glass so it sinks through the drink.
  8. Garnish with a lemon wheel on the rim.
