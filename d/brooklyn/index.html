STIRRED · COUPE

Brooklyn

Rye and dry vermouth with maraschino and a bittersweet orange amaro. The Manhattan's drier cousin from the other side of the river, spicy and quietly bitter.

Ingredients

  • 2 oz rye whiskey
  • 1 oz dry vermouth
  • ¼ oz maraschino liqueur
  • ¼ oz amaro, Amer Picon, or another bittersweet orange amaro

Steps

  1. Fill the mixing glass with ice.
  2. Add 2 oz rye whiskey to the mixing glass.
  3. Add 1 oz dry vermouth to the mixing glass.
  4. Add ¼ oz maraschino liqueur to the mixing glass.
  5. Add ¼ oz amaro (Amer Picon, or another bittersweet orange amaro) to the mixing glass.
  6. Stir with a bar spoon for about 30 seconds, until well chilled.
  7. Strain into a chilled coupe.
  8. Drop in a cocktail cherry.
