SHAKEN · COUPE

Casino Royale

Gin, lemon and maraschino shaken hard with an egg yolk until silky. A rich, custard-soft sour with a cherry-pit perfume and a dash of orange bitters.

Ingredients

  • 2 oz gin
  • ½ oz fresh lemon juice
  • ¼ oz maraschino liqueur
  • 1 dash orange bitters
  • ½ oz beaten egg, the yolk only

Steps

  1. Fill the shaker with ice.
  2. Add 2 oz gin to the shaker.
  3. Add ½ oz fresh lemon juice to the shaker.
  4. Add ¼ oz maraschino liqueur to the shaker.
  5. Add 1 dash orange bitters to the shaker.
  6. Add ½ oz beaten egg (the yolk only) to the shaker.
  7. Cap the shaker and shake hard for about 12 seconds.
  8. Strain into a chilled coupe.
  9. Express a lemon peel over the drink and drop it in.
