BUILT · HIGHBALL

The Jimmy Conway

Irish whiskey and amaretto poured over ice and lengthened with cranberry juice. An easy, sociable long drink: almond sweetness and tart berry, with a soft whiskey warmth underneath.

Ingredients

  • 1½ oz Irish whiskey
  • 1 oz amaretto
  • 4 oz cranberry juice

Steps

  1. Add ice cubes to a highball glass.
  2. Add 1½ oz Irish whiskey to the highball glass.
  3. Add 1 oz amaretto to the highball glass.
  4. Add 4 oz cranberry juice to the highball glass.
  5. Give it a gentle stir.
