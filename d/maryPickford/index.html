SHAKEN · COUPE

Mary Pickford

Rum and pineapple with maraschino and grenadine, named for the silent-film star.

Ingredients

  • 1½ oz white rum
  • 1½ oz pineapple juice
  • ¼ oz maraschino liqueur
  • ¼ oz pomegranate grenadine

Steps

  1. Fill the shaker with ice.
  2. Add 1½ oz white rum to the shaker.
  3. Add 1½ oz pineapple juice to the shaker.
  4. Add ¼ oz maraschino liqueur to the shaker.
  5. Add ¼ oz pomegranate grenadine to the shaker.
  6. Cap the shaker and shake hard for about 12 seconds.
  7. Strain into a chilled coupe.
  8. Drop in a cocktail cherry.
