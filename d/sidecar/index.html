SHAKEN · COUPE

Sidecar

Cognac and orange liqueur brightened with lemon, in a sugar-rimmed coupe.

Ingredients

  • 2 oz cognac
  • ¾ oz Cointreau
  • ¾ oz fresh lemon juice

Steps

  1. Wet the rim of a coupe and roll it in sugar.
  2. Fill the shaker with ice.
  3. Add 2 oz cognac to the shaker.
  4. Add ¾ oz Cointreau to the shaker.
  5. Add ¾ oz fresh lemon juice to the shaker.
  6. Cap the shaker and shake hard for about 12 seconds.
  7. Strain into the coupe.
  8. Express an orange peel over the drink and drop it in.
