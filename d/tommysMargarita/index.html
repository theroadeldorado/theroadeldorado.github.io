Tommy's Margarita

A margarita without the orange liqueur: tequila, fresh lime and agave over ice.

Ingredients

  • 2 oz blanco tequila
  • 1 oz fresh lime juice
  • ½ oz agave syrup

Steps

  1. Fill the shaker with ice. Add ice cubes to a rocks glass.
  2. Add 2 oz blanco tequila to the shaker.
  3. Add 1 oz fresh lime juice to the shaker.
  4. Add ½ oz agave syrup to the shaker.
  5. Cap the shaker and shake hard for about 12 seconds.
  6. Strain into the rocks glass.
  7. Garnish with a lime wedge on the rim.
