SHAKEN · HIGHBALL

Vampiro

Tequila with a homemade sangrita of tomato, fresh orange and lime, honey and a little heat, in a salt-rimmed glass.

Ingredients

  • 1½ oz blanco tequila
  • 2 oz tomato juice
  • 1 oz fresh orange juice
  • ½ oz fresh lime juice
  • ¼ oz honey syrup
  • 2 dashes hot sauce
  • 1 dash Worcestershire sauce

Steps

  1. Wet the rim of a highball glass and roll it in salt.
  2. Fill the shaker with ice. Add ice cubes to the highball glass.
  3. Add 1½ oz blanco tequila to the shaker.
  4. Add 2 oz tomato juice to the shaker.
  5. Add 1 oz fresh orange juice to the shaker.
  6. Add ½ oz fresh lime juice to the shaker.
  7. Add ¼ oz honey syrup to the shaker.
  8. Add 2 dashes hot sauce to the shaker.
  9. Add 1 dash Worcestershire sauce to the shaker.
  10. Cap the shaker and shake hard for about 12 seconds.
  11. Strain into the highball glass.
  12. Garnish with a lime wedge on the rim.
