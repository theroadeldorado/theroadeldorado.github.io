Bellini

Peach purée topped with Prosecco. Venice's brunch drink.

Ingredients

  • 1½ oz peach purée
  • 4 oz Prosecco

Steps

  1. Add 1½ oz peach purée to a flute.
  2. Top with 4 oz Prosecco.
  3. Give it a gentle stir.
