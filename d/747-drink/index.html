BUILT · HIGHBALL

747 Drink

Vodka and cranberry sharpened with fresh lime and lemon, then lifted with soda. Crisp, tart and built for takeoff.

Ingredients

  • 1½ oz vodka
  • 1½ oz cranberry juice
  • ½ oz fresh lime juice
  • ¼ oz fresh lemon juice
  • ½ oz simple syrup
  • Soda water, to top

Steps

  1. Add ice cubes to a highball glass.
  2. Add 1½ oz vodka to the highball glass.
  3. Add 1½ oz cranberry juice to the highball glass.
  4. Add ½ oz fresh lime juice to the highball glass.
  5. Add ¼ oz fresh lemon juice to the highball glass.
  6. Add ½ oz simple syrup to the highball glass.
  7. Top with soda water.
  8. Give it a gentle stir.
  9. Garnish with a lime wedge on the rim.
