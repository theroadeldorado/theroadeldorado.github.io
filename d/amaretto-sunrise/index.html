Amaretto Sunrise

Amaretto and fresh orange juice over ice, with grenadine sinking to the bottom. The Tequila Sunrise made softer and nuttier.

Ingredients

  • 1½ oz amaretto
  • 4 oz fresh orange juice
  • ½ oz pomegranate grenadine, poured last so it sinks

Steps

  1. Add ice cubes to a highball glass.
  2. Add 1½ oz amaretto to the highball glass.
  3. Add 4 oz fresh orange juice to the highball glass.
  4. Slowly pour ½ oz pomegranate grenadine down the inside of the highball glass so it sinks to the bottom.
  5. Garnish with an orange wheel on the rim. Add a straw.
