Gin Sour

Gin, fresh lemon and sugar shaken hard and strained into a stemmed glass with a slice of orange. The plain gin sour Jerry Thomas was printing by 1862: clean, tart and juniper-bright.

Ingredients

  • ¾ oz simple syrup
  • ¾ oz fresh lemon juice
  • 2 oz gin

Steps

  1. Fill the shaker with ice.
  2. Add ¾ oz simple syrup to the shaker.
  3. Add ¾ oz fresh lemon juice to the shaker.
  4. Add 2 oz gin to the shaker.
  5. Cap the shaker and shake hard for about 12 seconds.
  6. Strain into a chilled coupe.
  7. Garnish with an orange slice, a lemon wheel and a cherry.
