Lone Tree

Gin with sweet and dry vermouth in equal thirds and a couple of dashes of orange bitters, from the Savoy book of 1930. Softer than a Martini, drier than a Gin & It, and quietly aromatic.

Ingredients

  • 2 dashes orange bitters
  • 1 oz sweet vermouth
  • 1 oz dry vermouth
  • 1 oz gin

Steps

  1. Fill the mixing glass with ice.
  2. Add 2 dashes orange bitters to the mixing glass.
  3. Add 1 oz sweet vermouth to the mixing glass.
  4. Add 1 oz dry vermouth to the mixing glass.
  5. Add 1 oz gin to the mixing glass.
  6. Stir with a bar spoon for about 30 seconds, until well chilled.
  7. Strain into a chilled coupe.
  8. Express an orange peel over the drink and drop it in.
