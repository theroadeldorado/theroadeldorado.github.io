BUILT · ROCKS

Addington

Sweet and dry vermouth in equal parts over ice, with a splash of soda and a twist of orange. A light, aromatic Savoy aperitif.

Ingredients

  • 1½ oz sweet vermouth
  • 1½ oz dry vermouth
  • Soda water, to top

Steps

  1. Add ice cubes to a rocks glass.
  2. Add 1½ oz sweet vermouth to the rocks glass.
  3. Add 1½ oz dry vermouth to the rocks glass.
  4. Top with soda water.
  5. Give it a gentle stir.
  6. Express an orange peel over the drink and drop it in.
