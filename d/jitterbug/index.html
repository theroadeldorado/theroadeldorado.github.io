SHAKEN · HIGHBALL

Jitterbug

Gin and vodka shaken with lime, sugar and a little grenadine, then topped with soda in a sugar-rimmed glass. Tart, fizzy and stronger than its dance-hall name lets on.

Ingredients

  • 1½ oz gin
  • ¾ oz vodka
  • ¼ oz pomegranate grenadine
  • ¾ oz fresh lime juice
  • ½ oz simple syrup
  • Soda water, to top

Steps

  1. Wet the rim of a highball glass and roll it in sugar.
  2. Fill the shaker with ice. Add ice cubes to the highball glass.
  3. Add 1½ oz gin to the shaker.
  4. Add ¾ oz vodka to the shaker.
  5. Add ¼ oz pomegranate grenadine to the shaker.
  6. Add ¾ oz fresh lime juice to the shaker.
  7. Add ½ oz simple syrup to the shaker.
  8. Cap the shaker and shake hard for about 12 seconds.
  9. Strain into the highball glass.
  10. Top with soda water.
  11. Garnish with a lime wheel on the rim.
