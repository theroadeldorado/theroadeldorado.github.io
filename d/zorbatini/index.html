STIRRED · MARTINI

Zorbatini

Vodka stirred ice-cold with a splash of anise spirit standing in for Greek ouzo. A Martini with a Mediterranean accent: clean and bracing, with a cool licorice finish.

Ingredients

  • 1¾ oz vodka
  • ¼ oz pastis

Steps

  1. Fill the mixing glass with ice.
  2. Add 1¾ oz vodka to the mixing glass.
  3. Add ¼ oz pastis to the mixing glass.
  4. Stir with a bar spoon for about 30 seconds, until well chilled.
  5. Strain into a chilled martini glass.
