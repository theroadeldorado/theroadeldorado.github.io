Brandy Sour

Brandy and lemon with a little sugar and a dash of curaçao, the way Jerry Thomas wrote it in 1887. Tart, fruity and warming.

Ingredients

  • 2 oz cognac
  • ¾ oz fresh lemon juice
  • ¼ oz simple syrup
  • ¼ oz orange curaçao

Steps

  1. Fill the shaker with ice.
  2. Add 2 oz cognac to the shaker.
  3. Add ¾ oz fresh lemon juice to the shaker.
  4. Add ¼ oz simple syrup to the shaker.
  5. Add ¼ oz orange curaçao to the shaker.
  6. Cap the shaker and shake hard for about 12 seconds.
  7. Strain into a chilled coupe.
  8. Garnish with a cherry and a lemon wheel.
