Royal Fizz

Gin, fresh lemon and sugar shaken hard with a whole egg, then lengthened with soda. The Savoy's richest fizz: creamy, tart and gently sparkling.

Ingredients

  • 2 oz gin
  • ¾ oz fresh lemon juice
  • ½ oz simple syrup
  • 1½ oz beaten egg
  • 3 oz soda water

Steps

  1. Fill the shaker with ice.
  2. Add 2 oz gin to the shaker.
  3. Add ¾ oz fresh lemon juice to the shaker.
  4. Add ½ oz simple syrup to the shaker.
  5. Add 1½ oz beaten egg to the shaker.
  6. Cap the shaker and shake hard for about 12 seconds.
  7. Strain into a chilled highball glass.
  8. Top with 3 oz soda water.
