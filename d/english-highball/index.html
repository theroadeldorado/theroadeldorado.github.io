English Highball

Gin, brandy and sweet vermouth in equal parts over ice, topped with soda and a lemon twist, from Old Mr. Boston. A light, herbal long drink.

Ingredients

  • ¾ oz gin
  • ¾ oz cognac
  • ¾ oz sweet vermouth
  • Soda water, to top

Steps

  1. Add ice cubes to a highball glass.
  2. Add ¾ oz gin to the highball glass.
  3. Add ¾ oz cognac to the highball glass.
  4. Add ¾ oz sweet vermouth to the highball glass.
  5. Top with soda water.
  6. Give it a gentle stir.
  7. Express a lemon peel over the drink and drop it in.
