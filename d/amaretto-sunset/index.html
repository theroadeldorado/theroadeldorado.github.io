Amaretto Sunset

Amaretto and a little triple sec stretched with apple juice over ice. Nutty, orchard-sweet and mellow, like an apple tart with almond crust.

Ingredients

  • 1½ oz amaretto
  • ½ oz Cointreau
  • 4 oz apple juice

Steps

  1. Add ice cubes to a highball glass.
  2. Add 1½ oz amaretto to the highball glass.
  3. Add ½ oz Cointreau to the highball glass.
  4. Add 4 oz apple juice to the highball glass.
  5. Give it a gentle stir.
  6. Garnish with an orange wheel on the rim.
