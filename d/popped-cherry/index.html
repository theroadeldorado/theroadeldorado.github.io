BUILT · HIGHBALL

Popped Cherry

Vodka and cherry liqueur built over ice with cranberry and fresh orange juice. A cheerful party highball: tart berry, sweet cherry and a bright citrus lift.

Ingredients

  • 1½ oz vodka
  • 1 oz cherry liqueur
  • 2 oz cranberry juice
  • 2 oz fresh orange juice

Steps

  1. Add ice cubes to a highball glass.
  2. Add 1½ oz vodka to the highball glass.
  3. Add 1 oz cherry liqueur to the highball glass.
  4. Add 2 oz cranberry juice to the highball glass.
  5. Add 2 oz fresh orange juice to the highball glass.
  6. Give it a gentle stir.
  7. Drop in a cocktail cherry.
