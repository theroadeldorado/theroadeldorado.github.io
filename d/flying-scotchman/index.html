SHAKEN · COUPE

Flying Scotchman

Scotch and sweet vermouth with bitters and a little sugar, shaken in the Savoy manner. A Rob Roy cousin, softer and rounder.

Ingredients

  • 1¼ oz sweet vermouth
  • 1½ oz blended Scotch
  • 3 dashes Angostura bitters
  • ¼ oz simple syrup

Steps

  1. Fill the shaker with ice.
  2. Add 1¼ oz sweet vermouth to the shaker.
  3. Add 1½ oz blended Scotch to the shaker.
  4. Add 3 dashes Angostura bitters to the shaker.
  5. Add ¼ oz simple syrup to the shaker.
  6. Cap the shaker and shake hard for about 12 seconds.
  7. Strain into a chilled coupe.
