BUILT · HIGHBALL

Screwdriver

Vodka and fresh orange juice over ice. Brunch's simplest drink.

Ingredients

  • 1¾ oz vodka
  • 3½ oz fresh orange juice

Steps

  1. Add ice cubes to a highball glass.
  2. Add 1¾ oz vodka to the highball glass.
  3. Add 3½ oz fresh orange juice to the highball glass.
  4. Give it a gentle stir.
  5. Garnish with an orange wheel on the rim.
