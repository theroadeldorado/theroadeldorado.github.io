SHAKEN · COUPE

9 1/2 Weeks

Citrus vodka with orange curaçao, fresh orange juice and a splash of strawberry liqueur. Bright, fruity and a little flirtatious, like the film it's named for.

Ingredients

  • 2 oz citrus vodka
  • ½ oz orange curaçao
  • ¼ oz strawberry liqueur
  • 1 oz fresh orange juice

Steps

  1. Fill the shaker with ice.
  2. Add 2 oz citrus vodka to the shaker.
  3. Add ½ oz orange curaçao to the shaker.
  4. Add ¼ oz strawberry liqueur to the shaker.
  5. Add 1 oz fresh orange juice to the shaker.
  6. Cap the shaker and shake hard for about 12 seconds.
  7. Strain into a chilled coupe.
  8. Express an orange peel over the drink and drop it in.
