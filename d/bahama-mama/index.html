SHAKEN · HIGHBALL

Bahama Mama

Dark rum and coconut rum shaken with pineapple, orange and pomegranate grenadine, as Nassau bartender Oswald Greenslade said he first made it. Sweet, fruity island punch.

Ingredients

  • 1¼ oz dark rum
  • 1¼ oz coconut rum
  • 1 oz pomegranate grenadine
  • 2 oz pineapple juice
  • 2 oz fresh orange juice

Steps

  1. Fill the shaker with ice. Add crushed ice to a highball glass.
  2. Add 1¼ oz dark rum to the shaker.
  3. Add 1¼ oz coconut rum to the shaker.
  4. Add 1 oz pomegranate grenadine to the shaker.
  5. Add 2 oz pineapple juice to the shaker.
  6. Add 2 oz fresh orange juice to the shaker.
  7. Cap the shaker and shake hard for about 12 seconds.
  8. Strain into the highball glass.
  9. Garnish with a pineapple wedge, an orange slice and a cherry. Add a straw.
