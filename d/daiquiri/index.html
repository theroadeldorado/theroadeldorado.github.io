SHAKEN · COUPE

Daiquiri

Rum, lime and sugar, shaken hard. Simple and perfect.

Ingredients

  • 2 oz white rum
  • 1 oz fresh lime juice
  • ¾ oz simple syrup

Steps

  1. Fill the shaker with ice.
  2. Add 2 oz white rum to the shaker.
  3. Add 1 oz fresh lime juice to the shaker.
  4. Add ¾ oz simple syrup to the shaker.
  5. Cap the shaker and shake hard for about 12 seconds.
  6. Strain into a chilled coupe.
  7. Garnish with a lime wheel on the rim.
