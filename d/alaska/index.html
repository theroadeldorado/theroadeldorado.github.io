STIRRED · COUPE

Alaska

Gin and yellow Chartreuse, three to one, with orange bitters. A Savoy classic that's honeyed, herbal and quietly strong.

Ingredients

  • 2¼ oz gin
  • ¾ oz Yellow Chartreuse
  • 2 dashes orange bitters

Steps

  1. Fill the mixing glass with ice.
  2. Add 2¼ oz gin to the mixing glass.
  3. Add ¾ oz Yellow Chartreuse to the mixing glass.
  4. Add 2 dashes orange bitters to the mixing glass.
  5. Stir with a bar spoon for about 30 seconds, until well chilled.
  6. Strain into a chilled coupe.
  7. Express a lemon peel over the drink and drop it in.
