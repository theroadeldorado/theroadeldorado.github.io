Espresso Martini

Vodka and fresh espresso shaken to a creamy foam.

Ingredients

  • 2 oz vodka
  • ½ oz coffee liqueur
  • 1 oz espresso
  • ¼ oz simple syrup

Steps

  1. Fill the shaker with ice.
  2. Add 2 oz vodka to the shaker.
  3. Add ½ oz coffee liqueur to the shaker.
  4. Add 1 oz espresso to the shaker.
  5. Add ¼ oz simple syrup to the shaker.
  6. Cap the shaker and shake hard for about 12 seconds.
  7. Strain into a chilled martini glass.
  8. Float three coffee beans on top.
