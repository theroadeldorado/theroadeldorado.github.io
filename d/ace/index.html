SHAKEN · MARTINI

Ace

Gin shaken with grenadine, cream, milk and egg white into a soft, frothy after-dinner drink with a pomegranate edge.

Ingredients

  • 2 oz gin
  • ½ oz pomegranate grenadine
  • ½ oz heavy cream
  • ½ oz milk
  • ½ oz egg white

Steps

  1. Fill the shaker with ice.
  2. Add 2 oz gin to the shaker.
  3. Add ½ oz pomegranate grenadine to the shaker.
  4. Add ½ oz heavy cream to the shaker.
  5. Add ½ oz milk to the shaker.
  6. Add ½ oz egg white to the shaker.
  7. Cap the shaker and shake hard for about 12 seconds.
  8. Strain into a chilled martini glass.
