Vodka and Tonic

Vodka and tonic over plenty of ice with a lime wedge. Cleaner and more neutral than its gin cousin, so the tonic's bitter quinine and the squeeze of lime do the talking.

Ingredients

  • 2 oz vodka
  • 4 oz tonic water

Steps

  1. Add ice cubes to a highball glass.
  2. Add 2 oz vodka to the highball glass.
  3. Top with 4 oz tonic water.
  4. Give it a gentle stir.
  5. Garnish with a lime wedge on the rim.
