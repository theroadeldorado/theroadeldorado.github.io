STIRRED · ROCKS

Broadside

Overproof rum stirred with a little Scotch, a dash of bitters and a whisper of absinthe, over one big cube. Fiery, smoky and not for the faint-hearted.

Ingredients

  • 1 oz overproof rum
  • ½ oz blended Scotch
  • 1 dash Angostura bitters
  • 1 barspoon absinthe

Steps

  1. Fill the mixing glass with ice. Add a big ice cube to a rocks glass.
  2. Add 1 oz overproof rum to the mixing glass.
  3. Add ½ oz blended Scotch to the mixing glass.
  4. Add 1 dash Angostura bitters to the mixing glass.
  5. Add 1 barspoon absinthe to the mixing glass.
  6. Stir with a bar spoon for about 30 seconds, until well chilled.
  7. Strain into the rocks glass.
