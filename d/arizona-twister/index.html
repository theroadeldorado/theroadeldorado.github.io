SHAKEN · HURRICANE

Arizona Twister

Vodka, tequila and coconut rum shaken with orange, pineapple and cream of coconut, with a touch of grenadine. A big, sweet desert-resort drink that hides its strength.

Ingredients

  • 1 oz vodka
  • 1 oz coconut rum
  • 1 oz blanco tequila
  • 1½ oz fresh orange juice
  • 1½ oz pineapple juice
  • ½ oz cream of coconut
  • ¼ oz pomegranate grenadine

Steps

  1. Fill the shaker with ice. Add crushed ice to a hurricane glass.
  2. Add 1 oz vodka to the shaker.
  3. Add 1 oz coconut rum to the shaker.
  4. Add 1 oz blanco tequila to the shaker.
  5. Add 1½ oz fresh orange juice to the shaker.
  6. Add 1½ oz pineapple juice to the shaker.
  7. Add ½ oz cream of coconut to the shaker.
  8. Add ¼ oz pomegranate grenadine to the shaker.
  9. Cap the shaker and shake hard for about 12 seconds.
  10. Strain into the hurricane glass.
  11. Garnish with a pineapple wedge on the rim. Add a straw.
