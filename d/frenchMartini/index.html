SHAKEN · MARTINI

French Martini

Vodka, raspberry liqueur and pineapple, shaken hard for a frothy top.

Ingredients

  • 1½ oz vodka
  • ½ oz raspberry liqueur
  • ½ oz pineapple juice

Steps

  1. Fill the shaker with ice.
  2. Add 1½ oz vodka to the shaker.
  3. Add ½ oz raspberry liqueur to the shaker.
  4. Add ½ oz pineapple juice to the shaker.
  5. Cap the shaker and shake hard for about 12 seconds.
  6. Strain into a chilled martini glass.
  7. Express a lemon peel over the drink and drop it in.
