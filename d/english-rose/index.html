SHAKEN · COUPE

English Rose

Gin, apricot brandy and dry vermouth with a little pomegranate grenadine and lemon, shaken and served up with a cherry. Mr. Boston's fruity, gently sweet sipper.

Ingredients

  • 1½ oz gin
  • ¾ oz apricot brandy
  • ¾ oz dry vermouth
  • ¼ oz pomegranate grenadine
  • ¼ oz fresh lemon juice

Steps

  1. Wet the rim of a coupe and roll it in sugar.
  2. Fill the shaker with ice.
  3. Add 1½ oz gin to the shaker.
  4. Add ¾ oz apricot brandy to the shaker.
  5. Add ¾ oz dry vermouth to the shaker.
  6. Add ¼ oz pomegranate grenadine to the shaker.
  7. Add ¼ oz fresh lemon juice to the shaker.
  8. Cap the shaker and shake hard for about 12 seconds.
  9. Strain into the coupe.
  10. Drop in a cocktail cherry.
