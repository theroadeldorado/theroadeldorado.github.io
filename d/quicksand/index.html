Quicksand

A shot of sambuca stretched with fresh orange juice over ice. Licorice and orange turn out to be old friends: sweet, aniseed-scented and easy to sip.

Ingredients

  • 1 oz sambuca
  • 5 oz fresh orange juice

Steps

  1. Add ice cubes to a highball glass.
  2. Add 1 oz sambuca to the highball glass.
  3. Add 5 oz fresh orange juice to the highball glass.
  4. Give it a gentle stir.
  5. Garnish with an orange wheel on the rim.
