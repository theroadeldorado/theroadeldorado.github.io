Elderflower Caipirinha

Lime crushed with elderflower liqueur instead of sugar, then cachaça over crushed ice. The Brazilian classic with a soft, blossomy sweetness.

Ingredients

  • ½ lime, cut into wedges
  • ¾ oz elderflower liqueur
  • 2 oz cachaça

Steps

  1. Muddle ½ lime (cut into wedges) in a rocks glass.
  2. Add crushed ice to the rocks glass.
  3. Add ¾ oz elderflower liqueur to the rocks glass.
  4. Add 2 oz cachaça to the rocks glass.
  5. Give it a gentle stir.
  6. Garnish with a lime wheel on the rim.
