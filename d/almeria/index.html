SHAKEN · COUPE

Almeria

Dark rum and coffee liqueur shaken hard with egg white. Rich and roasty under a silky foam, like an after-dinner coffee you can sip cold.

Ingredients

  • 2 oz dark rum
  • 1 oz coffee liqueur
  • ¾ oz egg white

Steps

  1. Fill the shaker with ice.
  2. Add 2 oz dark rum to the shaker.
  3. Add 1 oz coffee liqueur to the shaker.
  4. Add ¾ oz egg white to the shaker.
  5. Cap the shaker and shake hard for about 12 seconds.
  6. Strain into a chilled coupe.
