Cuba Libre

Rum and cola brightened with fresh lime. The lime is what makes it more than a rum and coke.

Ingredients

  • 1¾ oz white rum
  • ¼ oz fresh lime juice
  • Cola, to top

Steps

  1. Add ice cubes to a highball glass.
  2. Add 1¾ oz white rum to the highball glass.
  3. Add ¼ oz fresh lime juice to the highball glass.
  4. Top with cola.
  5. Give it a gentle stir.
  6. Garnish with a lime wedge on the rim.
