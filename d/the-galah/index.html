The Galah

Dark rum, Campari and crème de banane shaken with pineapple and fresh lime and served tall over ice. Named for the noisy Australian cockatoo: tropical and fruity at first, with a bitter bite at the finish.

Ingredients

  • 1¼ oz dark rum
  • 1 oz Campari
  • ½ oz crème de banane
  • 3 oz pineapple juice
  • ½ oz fresh lime juice

Steps

  1. Fill the shaker with ice. Add ice cubes to a highball glass.
  2. Add 1¼ oz dark rum to the shaker.
  3. Add 1 oz Campari to the shaker.
  4. Add ½ oz crème de banane to the shaker.
  5. Add 3 oz pineapple juice to the shaker.
  6. Add ½ oz fresh lime juice to the shaker.
  7. Cap the shaker and shake hard for about 12 seconds.
  8. Strain into the highball glass.
  9. Garnish with a pineapple wedge on the rim.
