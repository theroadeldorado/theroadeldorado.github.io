Dragonfly

Gin and a squeeze of lime over ice, lengthened with ginger ale. An easy, gently spiced highball for a warm evening.

Ingredients

  • 1½ oz gin
  • ½ oz fresh lime juice
  • 4 oz ginger ale

Steps

  1. Add ice cubes to a highball glass.
  2. Add 1½ oz gin to the highball glass.
  3. Add ½ oz fresh lime juice to the highball glass.
  4. Top with 4 oz ginger ale.
  5. Give it a gentle stir.
  6. Garnish with a lime wedge on the rim.
