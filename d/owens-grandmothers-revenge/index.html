Owen's Grandmother's Revenge

Bourbon, fresh lemon and sugar over ice, filled up with cold lager. A whiskey shandy from the backyard-pitcher school, made by the glass with real lemon in place of frozen concentrate.

Ingredients

  • 1½ oz bourbon
  • 1 oz fresh lemon juice
  • ¾ oz simple syrup
  • 4 oz lager

Steps

  1. Add ice cubes to a highball glass.
  2. Add 1½ oz bourbon to the highball glass.
  3. Add 1 oz fresh lemon juice to the highball glass.
  4. Add ¾ oz simple syrup to the highball glass.
  5. Top with 4 oz lager.
  6. Give it a gentle stir.
  7. Garnish with a lemon wheel on the rim.
