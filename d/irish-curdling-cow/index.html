Irish Curdling Cow

Irish cream, bourbon and vodka over ice, filled up with fresh orange juice. A daft name for a sweet, orange-and-cream brunch drink; stir it well and drink it cold.

Ingredients

  • ¾ oz Irish cream
  • ¾ oz bourbon
  • ¾ oz vodka
  • 3 oz fresh orange juice

Steps

  1. Add ice cubes to a highball glass.
  2. Add ¾ oz Irish cream to the highball glass.
  3. Add ¾ oz bourbon to the highball glass.
  4. Add ¾ oz vodka to the highball glass.
  5. Add 3 oz fresh orange juice to the highball glass.
  6. Give it a gentle stir.
  7. Garnish with an orange wheel on the rim.
