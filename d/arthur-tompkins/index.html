SHAKEN · COUPE

Arthur Tompkins

Gin with Grand Marnier and just a little lemon, shaken hard and finished with a lemon twist. A strong gin sour that leans dry and orange-scented.

Ingredients

  • 2 oz gin
  • ½ oz Grand Marnier
  • ¼ oz fresh lemon juice

Steps

  1. Fill the shaker with ice.
  2. Add 2 oz gin to the shaker.
  3. Add ½ oz Grand Marnier to the shaker.
  4. Add ¼ oz fresh lemon juice to the shaker.
  5. Cap the shaker and shake hard for about 12 seconds.
  6. Strain into a chilled coupe.
  7. Express a lemon peel over the drink and drop it in.
