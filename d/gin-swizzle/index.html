Gin Swizzle

Lime, sugar, bitters and gin swizzled over crushed ice and topped with soda. Old Mr. Boston's long, tart cooler, served with its swizzle stick still in the glass.

Ingredients

  • 1 oz fresh lime juice
  • ¾ oz simple syrup
  • 2 dashes Angostura bitters
  • 2 oz gin
  • Soda water, to top

Steps

  1. Add crushed ice to a highball glass.
  2. Add 1 oz fresh lime juice to the highball glass.
  3. Add ¾ oz simple syrup to the highball glass.
  4. Add 2 dashes Angostura bitters to the highball glass.
  5. Add 2 oz gin to the highball glass.
  6. Top with soda water.
  7. Give it a gentle stir.
  8. Garnish with a sprig of mint. Add a stirrer.
