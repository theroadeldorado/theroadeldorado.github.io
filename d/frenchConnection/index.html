BUILT · ROCKS

French Connection

Cognac and amaretto over ice: the Godfather's French cousin.

Ingredients

  • 1¼ oz cognac
  • 1¼ oz amaretto

Steps

  1. Add a big ice cube to a rocks glass.
  2. Add 1¼ oz cognac to the rocks glass.
  3. Add 1¼ oz amaretto to the rocks glass.
  4. Give it a gentle stir.
