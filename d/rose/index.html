STIRRED · COUPE

Rose

Dry vermouth and kirsch, two to one, stirred with a little raspberry syrup and served up. The signature drink of 1920s Paris: dry and delicate, with a cherry-stone perfume.

Ingredients

  • 2 oz dry vermouth
  • 1 oz kirsch
  • ¼ oz raspberry syrup

Steps

  1. Fill the mixing glass with ice.
  2. Add 2 oz dry vermouth to the mixing glass.
  3. Add 1 oz kirsch to the mixing glass.
  4. Add ¼ oz raspberry syrup to the mixing glass.
  5. Stir with a bar spoon for about 30 seconds, until well chilled.
  6. Strain into a chilled coupe.
  7. Drop in a cocktail cherry.
