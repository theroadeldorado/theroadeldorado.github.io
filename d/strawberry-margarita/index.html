Strawberry Margarita

Fresh strawberries muddled with blanco tequila, orange liqueur, lime and a little sugar, shaken and fine strained into a salt-rimmed coupe. Bright berry up front, then agave and lime.

Ingredients

  • 5 strawberries
  • 1½ oz blanco tequila
  • ¾ oz Cointreau
  • ¾ oz fresh lime juice
  • ½ oz simple syrup

Steps

  1. Wet the rim of a coupe and roll it in salt.
  2. Muddle 5 strawberries in the shaker.
  3. Fill the shaker with ice.
  4. Add 1½ oz blanco tequila to the shaker.
  5. Add ¾ oz Cointreau to the shaker.
  6. Add ¾ oz fresh lime juice to the shaker.
  7. Add ½ oz simple syrup to the shaker.
  8. Cap the shaker and shake hard for about 12 seconds.
  9. Strain into the coupe.
  10. Garnish with a strawberry on the rim.
