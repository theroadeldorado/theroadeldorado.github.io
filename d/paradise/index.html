SHAKEN · COUPE

Paradise

Gin, apricot brandy and fresh orange juice. Fruity, but still dry.

Ingredients

  • 1½ oz gin
  • ¾ oz apricot brandy
  • ¾ oz fresh orange juice

Steps

  1. Fill the shaker with ice.
  2. Add 1½ oz gin to the shaker.
  3. Add ¾ oz apricot brandy to the shaker.
  4. Add ¾ oz fresh orange juice to the shaker.
  5. Cap the shaker and shake hard for about 12 seconds.
  6. Strain into a chilled coupe.
  7. Express an orange peel over the drink and drop it in.
