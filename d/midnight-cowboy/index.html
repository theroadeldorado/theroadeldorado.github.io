SHAKEN · COUPE

Midnight Cowboy

Bourbon and dark rum shaken with a splash of cream and served up. It shares a name with the 1969 film: strong, smooth and rich, with oak and molasses under a soft, milky edge.

Ingredients

  • 2 oz bourbon
  • 1 oz dark rum
  • ¾ oz heavy cream

Steps

  1. Fill the shaker with ice.
  2. Add 2 oz bourbon to the shaker.
  3. Add 1 oz dark rum to the shaker.
  4. Add ¾ oz heavy cream to the shaker.
  5. Cap the shaker and shake hard for about 12 seconds.
  6. Strain into a chilled coupe.
