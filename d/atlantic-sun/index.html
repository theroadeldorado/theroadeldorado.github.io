SHAKEN · HIGHBALL

Atlantic Sun

Vodka and Southern Comfort shaken with passion fruit syrup and fresh lemon, then lifted with a splash of soda. Tangy, tropical and sunny.

Ingredients

  • 1 oz vodka
  • 1 oz Southern Comfort
  • ¾ oz passion fruit syrup
  • 1 oz fresh lemon juice
  • ¼ oz simple syrup
  • Soda water, to top

Steps

  1. Fill the shaker with ice. Add ice cubes to a highball glass.
  2. Add 1 oz vodka to the shaker.
  3. Add 1 oz Southern Comfort to the shaker.
  4. Add ¾ oz passion fruit syrup to the shaker.
  5. Add 1 oz fresh lemon juice to the shaker.
  6. Add ¼ oz simple syrup to the shaker.
  7. Cap the shaker and shake hard for about 12 seconds.
  8. Strain into the highball glass.
  9. Top with soda water.
  10. Garnish with an orange wheel on the rim.
