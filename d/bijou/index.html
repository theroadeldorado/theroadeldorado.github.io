STIRRED · NICK & NORA

Bijou

Gin, sweet vermouth and green Chartreuse in equal parts, stirred very cold. Harry Johnson's jewel of 1900: herbal, rich and strong.

Ingredients

  • 1 oz gin
  • 1 oz sweet vermouth
  • 1 oz Green Chartreuse
  • 1 dash orange bitters

Steps

  1. Fill the mixing glass with ice.
  2. Add 1 oz gin to the mixing glass.
  3. Add 1 oz sweet vermouth to the mixing glass.
  4. Add 1 oz Green Chartreuse to the mixing glass.
  5. Add 1 dash orange bitters to the mixing glass.
  6. Stir with a bar spoon for about 30 seconds, until well chilled.
  7. Strain into a chilled Nick & Nora glass.
  8. Drop in a cocktail cherry.
