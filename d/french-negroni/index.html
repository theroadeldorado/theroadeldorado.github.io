French Negroni

A Negroni with Lillet Blanc in place of Campari, stirred and served up. Gentler and more floral, with honeyed orange where the bitterness would be.

Ingredients

  • 1 oz gin
  • 1 oz Lillet Blanc
  • 1 oz sweet vermouth

Steps

  1. Fill the mixing glass with ice.
  2. Add 1 oz gin to the mixing glass.
  3. Add 1 oz Lillet Blanc to the mixing glass.
  4. Add 1 oz sweet vermouth to the mixing glass.
  5. Stir with a bar spoon for about 30 seconds, until well chilled.
  6. Strain into a chilled martini glass.
  7. Express an orange peel over the drink and drop it in.
