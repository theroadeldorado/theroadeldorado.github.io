Gentleman's Club

Gin, brandy and sweet vermouth over ice with a splash of soda to open it up. A rich, strong sipper somewhere between a Manhattan and a highball.

Ingredients

  • 1½ oz gin
  • ¾ oz cognac
  • ¾ oz sweet vermouth
  • 1 oz soda water

Steps

  1. Add ice cubes to a rocks glass.
  2. Add 1½ oz gin to the rocks glass.
  3. Add ¾ oz cognac to the rocks glass.
  4. Add ¾ oz sweet vermouth to the rocks glass.
  5. Top with 1 oz soda water.
  6. Give it a gentle stir.
