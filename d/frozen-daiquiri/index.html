Frozen Daiquiri

The Daiquiri blended with crushed ice into a fine, snowy slush, rounded with a little maraschino. Sharp lime, clean rum and a chill you feel behind the eyes.

Ingredients

  • 2 oz white rum
  • ¾ oz fresh lime juice
  • 1 oz simple syrup
  • ¼ oz maraschino liqueur

Steps

  1. Blend 2 oz white rum, ¾ oz fresh lime juice, 1 oz simple syrup and ¼ oz maraschino liqueur with ½ cup of ice until smooth, then pour into a coupe.
  2. Garnish with a lime wheel and a cherry. Add a straw.
