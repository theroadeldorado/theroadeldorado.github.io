SHAKEN · ROCKS

Penicillin

Scotch, lemon, honey and ginger with a smoky float.

Ingredients

  • 2 oz blended Scotch
  • ¾ oz fresh lemon juice
  • ¾ oz honey-ginger syrup
  • ¼ oz Islay Scotch, floated on top

Steps

  1. Fill the shaker with ice. Add a big ice cube to a rocks glass.
  2. Add 2 oz blended Scotch to the shaker.
  3. Add ¾ oz fresh lemon juice to the shaker.
  4. Add ¾ oz honey-ginger syrup to the shaker.
  5. Cap the shaker and shake hard for about 12 seconds.
  6. Strain into the rocks glass.
  7. Over the back of a spoon, gently pour ¼ oz Islay Scotch onto the drink so it floats on top.
  8. Garnish with a lemon wheel on the rim.
