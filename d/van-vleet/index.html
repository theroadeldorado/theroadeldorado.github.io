SHAKEN · ROCKS

Van Vleet

Light rum shaken with fresh lemon and real maple syrup and poured over ice. A rum sour with a woodsy, caramel sweetness from the maple, tart enough to stay refreshing.

Ingredients

  • 2 oz white rum
  • ½ oz maple syrup
  • ¾ oz fresh lemon juice

Steps

  1. Fill the shaker with ice. Add ice cubes to a rocks glass.
  2. Add 2 oz white rum to the shaker.
  3. Add ½ oz maple syrup to the shaker.
  4. Add ¾ oz fresh lemon juice to the shaker.
  5. Cap the shaker and shake hard for about 12 seconds.
  6. Strain into the rocks glass.
