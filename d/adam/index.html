SHAKEN · COUPE

Adam

Dark rum shaken with fresh lemon and pomegranate grenadine. A rich, tart rum sour with a gentle fruity sweetness.

Ingredients

  • 2 oz dark rum
  • ¾ oz fresh lemon juice
  • ½ oz pomegranate grenadine

Steps

  1. Fill the shaker with ice.
  2. Add 2 oz dark rum to the shaker.
  3. Add ¾ oz fresh lemon juice to the shaker.
  4. Add ½ oz pomegranate grenadine to the shaker.
  5. Cap the shaker and shake hard for about 12 seconds.
  6. Strain into a chilled coupe.
