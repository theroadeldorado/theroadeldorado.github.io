BUILT · ROCKS

Bramble

Gin, lemon and sugar over crushed ice, with blackberry liqueur bleeding down through it.

Ingredients

  • 1¼ oz gin
  • ¾ oz fresh lemon juice
  • ¼ oz simple syrup
  • ½ oz crème de mûre, drizzled over the top

Steps

  1. Add crushed ice to a rocks glass.
  2. Add 1¼ oz gin to the rocks glass.
  3. Add ¾ oz fresh lemon juice to the rocks glass.
  4. Add ¼ oz simple syrup to the rocks glass.
  5. Give it a gentle stir.
  6. Slowly drizzle the crème de mûre over the ice in the rocks glass so it bleeds down through the drink.
  7. Garnish with a lemon wheel on the rim.
