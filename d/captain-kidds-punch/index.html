Captain Kidd's Punch

Dark rum, lime and sugar shaken with egg white and bitters, then finished with grated nutmeg. A frothy pirate's punch, spiced and tart.

Ingredients

  • 2 oz dark rum
  • 1 oz fresh lime juice
  • ¾ oz simple syrup
  • ¾ oz egg white
  • 1 dash Angostura bitters

Steps

  1. Fill the shaker with ice. Add ice cubes to a highball glass.
  2. Add 2 oz dark rum to the shaker.
  3. Add 1 oz fresh lime juice to the shaker.
  4. Add ¾ oz simple syrup to the shaker.
  5. Add ¾ oz egg white to the shaker.
  6. Add 1 dash Angostura bitters to the shaker.
  7. Cap the shaker and shake hard for about 12 seconds.
  8. Strain into the highball glass.
  9. Grate a little nutmeg over the top.
