Gin & Tonic

Gin and tonic, Spanish style in a big wine glass.

Ingredients

  • 2 oz gin
  • 4 oz tonic water

Steps

  1. Add ice cubes to a wine glass.
  2. Add 2 oz gin to the wine glass.
  3. Top with 4 oz tonic water.
  4. Give it a gentle stir.
  5. Garnish with a lime wedge on the rim.
