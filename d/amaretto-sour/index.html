Amaretto Sour

Amaretto shaken with plenty of fresh lemon, egg white and a dash of Angostura, served over ice with a cherry. Made fresh, it's tart, nutty and silky.

Ingredients

  • 2 oz amaretto
  • 1 oz fresh lemon juice
  • ¾ oz egg white
  • 1 dash Angostura bitters

Steps

  1. Fill the shaker with ice. Add ice cubes to a rocks glass.
  2. Add 2 oz amaretto to the shaker.
  3. Add 1 oz fresh lemon juice to the shaker.
  4. Add ¾ oz egg white to the shaker.
  5. Add 1 dash Angostura bitters to the shaker.
  6. Cap the shaker and shake hard for about 12 seconds.
  7. Strain into the rocks glass.
  8. Garnish with a cherry and a lemon wheel.
