Yellow Bird

White rum with Galliano's vanilla and anise, orange liqueur and fresh lime.

Ingredients

  • 1 oz white rum
  • ½ oz Galliano
  • ½ oz Cointreau
  • ½ oz fresh lime juice

Steps

  1. Fill the shaker with ice.
  2. Add 1 oz white rum to the shaker.
  3. Add ½ oz Galliano to the shaker.
  4. Add ½ oz Cointreau to the shaker.
  5. Add ½ oz fresh lime juice to the shaker.
  6. Cap the shaker and shake hard for about 12 seconds.
  7. Strain into a chilled coupe.
