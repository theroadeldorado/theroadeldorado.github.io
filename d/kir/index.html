Kir

Chilled dry white wine with a little crème de cassis. The Burgundy aperitif named for Canon Félix Kir, the mayor of Dijon who poured it for visitors: crisp and lightly sweet with blackcurrant.

Ingredients

  • ½ oz crème de cassis
  • 4½ oz white wine

Steps

  1. Add ½ oz crème de cassis to a wine glass.
  2. Add 4½ oz white wine to the wine glass.
  3. Give it a gentle stir.
