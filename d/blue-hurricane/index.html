SHAKEN · HURRICANE

Blue Hurricane

Two rums, passion fruit and blue curaçao, sharpened with fresh lemon and shaken long over ice. A Hurricane with an orange-peel twist in the flavor and a party in the glass.

Ingredients

  • 1½ oz white rum
  • ¾ oz dark rum
  • ½ oz passion fruit syrup
  • ½ oz blue curaçao
  • 1 oz fresh lemon juice
  • ¼ oz simple syrup

Steps

  1. Fill the shaker with ice. Add ice cubes to a hurricane glass.
  2. Add 1½ oz white rum to the shaker.
  3. Add ¾ oz dark rum to the shaker.
  4. Add ½ oz passion fruit syrup to the shaker.
  5. Add ½ oz blue curaçao to the shaker.
  6. Add 1 oz fresh lemon juice to the shaker.
  7. Add ¼ oz simple syrup to the shaker.
  8. Cap the shaker and shake hard for about 12 seconds.
  9. Strain into the hurricane glass.
  10. Finish with a paper umbrella. Add a straw.
