SHAKEN · HIGHBALL

Chicago Fizz

Port and Jamaica rum shaken with lemon, sugar and egg white, then lifted with soda. A Windy City fizz from before Prohibition: plummy, frothy and gently tart.

Ingredients

  • ¾ oz fresh lemon juice
  • ¼ oz simple syrup
  • ¾ oz egg white
  • 1 oz ruby port
  • 1 oz dark rum
  • Soda water, to top

Steps

  1. Fill the shaker with ice. Add ice cubes to a highball glass.
  2. Add ¾ oz fresh lemon juice to the shaker.
  3. Add ¼ oz simple syrup to the shaker.
  4. Add ¾ oz egg white to the shaker.
  5. Add 1 oz ruby port to the shaker.
  6. Add 1 oz dark rum to the shaker.
  7. Cap the shaker and shake hard for about 12 seconds.
  8. Strain into the highball glass.
  9. Top with soda water.
