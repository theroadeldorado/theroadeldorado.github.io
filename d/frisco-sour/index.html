Frisco Sour

Whiskey with Bénédictine for its sweetener and both lemon and lime, shaken and served up. Old Mr. Boston's herbal, honey-tinged whiskey sour.

Ingredients

  • ¼ oz fresh lemon juice
  • ½ oz fresh lime juice
  • ½ oz Bénédictine
  • 2 oz rye whiskey

Steps

  1. Fill the shaker with ice.
  2. Add ¼ oz fresh lemon juice to the shaker.
  3. Add ½ oz fresh lime juice to the shaker.
  4. Add ½ oz Bénédictine to the shaker.
  5. Add 2 oz rye whiskey to the shaker.
  6. Cap the shaker and shake hard for about 12 seconds.
  7. Strain into a chilled coupe.
  8. Garnish with a lemon wheel and a cherry.
