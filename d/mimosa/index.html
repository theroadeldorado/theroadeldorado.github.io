Mimosa

Orange juice and Champagne.

Ingredients

  • 2½ oz fresh orange juice
  • 3 oz Champagne

Steps

  1. Add 2½ oz fresh orange juice to a flute.
  2. Top with 3 oz Champagne.
  3. Give it a gentle stir.
  4. Express an orange peel over the drink and drop it in.
