SHAKEN · COUPE

Abbey

Gin, Lillet and fresh orange juice with a dash of orange bitters. A Savoy classic that's soft, aromatic and gently fruity.

Ingredients

  • 1½ oz gin
  • ¾ oz Lillet Blanc
  • ¾ oz fresh orange juice
  • 1 dash orange bitters

Steps

  1. Fill the shaker with ice.
  2. Add 1½ oz gin to the shaker.
  3. Add ¾ oz Lillet Blanc to the shaker.
  4. Add ¾ oz fresh orange juice to the shaker.
  5. Add 1 dash orange bitters to the shaker.
  6. Cap the shaker and shake hard for about 12 seconds.
  7. Strain into a chilled coupe.
  8. Drop in a cocktail cherry.
