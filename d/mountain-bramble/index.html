Mountain Bramble

Fresh blackberries crushed with lemon and sugar, then gin over crushed ice and a splash of soda. Jammy, tart and bright: a fizzy, fresh-fruit cousin of the Bramble.

Ingredients

  • 4 blackberries
  • ¾ oz fresh lemon juice
  • ¾ oz simple syrup
  • 2 oz gin
  • 1 oz soda water

Steps

  1. Muddle 4 blackberries in a rocks glass.
  2. Add crushed ice to the rocks glass.
  3. Add ¾ oz fresh lemon juice to the rocks glass.
  4. Add ¾ oz simple syrup to the rocks glass.
  5. Add 2 oz gin to the rocks glass.
  6. Top with 1 oz soda water.
  7. Give it a gentle stir.
  8. Garnish with a sprig of mint.
