SHAKEN · COUPE

Hemingway Special

The daiquiri Hemingway drank in Havana: no sugar, with fresh grapefruit and a little maraschino.

Ingredients

  • 2 oz white rum
  • 1¼ oz fresh grapefruit juice
  • ½ oz maraschino liqueur
  • ½ oz fresh lime juice

Steps

  1. Fill the shaker with ice.
  2. Add 2 oz white rum to the shaker.
  3. Add 1¼ oz fresh grapefruit juice to the shaker.
  4. Add ½ oz maraschino liqueur to the shaker.
  5. Add ½ oz fresh lime juice to the shaker.
  6. Cap the shaker and shake hard for about 12 seconds.
  7. Strain into a chilled coupe.
