The Laverstoke

Gin, elderflower and sweet vermouth built over ice in a big wine glass with a squeeze of lime, topped with tonic and finished with mint. Named for the Hampshire mill where Bombay Sapphire is made: floral, bittersweet and crisp.

Ingredients

  • 1¾ oz gin
  • ½ oz elderflower liqueur
  • ½ oz sweet vermouth
  • ¼ oz fresh lime juice
  • 2½ oz tonic water

Steps

  1. Add ice cubes to a wine glass.
  2. Add 1¾ oz gin to the wine glass.
  3. Add ½ oz elderflower liqueur to the wine glass.
  4. Add ½ oz sweet vermouth to the wine glass.
  5. Add ¼ oz fresh lime juice to the wine glass.
  6. Top with 2½ oz tonic water.
  7. Give it a gentle stir.
  8. Garnish with a sprig of mint.
