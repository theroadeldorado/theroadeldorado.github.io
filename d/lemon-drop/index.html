SHAKEN · MARTINI

Lemon Drop

Vodka, orange liqueur and fresh lemon shaken and served up in a sugar-rimmed glass. Sweet-tart like the candy it is named for; a San Francisco fern-bar favorite of the 1970s.

Ingredients

  • 1½ oz vodka
  • 1 oz Cointreau
  • ¾ oz fresh lemon juice

Steps

  1. Wet the rim of a martini glass and roll it in sugar.
  2. Fill the shaker with ice.
  3. Add 1½ oz vodka to the shaker.
  4. Add 1 oz Cointreau to the shaker.
  5. Add ¾ oz fresh lemon juice to the shaker.
  6. Cap the shaker and shake hard for about 12 seconds.
  7. Strain into the martini glass.
  8. Express a lemon peel over the drink and drop it in.
