Bacardi Cocktail

A daiquiri sweetened with pomegranate grenadine instead of sugar, first made to show off Bacardi's rum.

Ingredients

  • 1½ oz white rum
  • ¾ oz fresh lime juice
  • ¼ oz pomegranate grenadine

Steps

  1. Fill the shaker with ice.
  2. Add 1½ oz white rum to the shaker.
  3. Add ¾ oz fresh lime juice to the shaker.
  4. Add ¼ oz pomegranate grenadine to the shaker.
  5. Cap the shaker and shake hard for about 12 seconds.
  6. Strain into a chilled coupe.
