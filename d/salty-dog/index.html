Salty Dog

Vodka and fresh grapefruit juice over ice in a salt-rimmed glass. The salt tames the grapefruit's bitterness and makes the fruit taste sweeter; leave it off and you have a Greyhound.

Ingredients

  • 2 oz vodka
  • 4 oz fresh grapefruit juice

Steps

  1. Wet the rim of a highball glass and roll it in salt.
  2. Add ice cubes to the highball glass.
  3. Add 2 oz vodka to the highball glass.
  4. Add 4 oz fresh grapefruit juice to the highball glass.
  5. Give it a gentle stir.
