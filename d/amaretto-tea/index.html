Amaretto Tea

A mug of hot black tea sweetened with amaretto and capped with cool whipped cream. Gentle, nutty and warming on a cold afternoon.

Ingredients

  • 4½ oz black tea, freshly brewed and hot
  • 1½ oz amaretto
  • 1 oz heavy cream, lightly whipped, floated on top

Steps

  1. Add 4½ oz black tea (freshly brewed and hot) to an Irish coffee glass.
  2. Add 1½ oz amaretto to the Irish coffee glass.
  3. Stir to mix the amaretto into the tea.
  4. Whip the cream until it just thickens, then spoon it gently onto the drink so it floats.
