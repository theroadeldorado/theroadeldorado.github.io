Apple Highball

Applejack and cognac over ice with a squeeze of lime, lengthened with ginger ale. Two brandies make it rounder than the usual highball, with a gentle spice.

Ingredients

  • 1 oz applejack
  • 1 oz cognac
  • ½ oz fresh lime juice
  • 4 oz ginger ale

Steps

  1. Add ice cubes to a highball glass.
  2. Add 1 oz applejack to the highball glass.
  3. Add 1 oz cognac to the highball glass.
  4. Add ½ oz fresh lime juice to the highball glass.
  5. Top with 4 oz ginger ale.
  6. Give it a gentle stir.
  7. Garnish with a lime wedge on the rim.
