BLENDED · HURRICANE

Zombie

Three rums, one of them overproof, flash-blended with lime, falernum, grapefruit, grenadine, bitters and a few drops of anise. Don the Beachcomber's 1934 knockout: fruity and spiced, and far stronger than it tastes.

Ingredients

  • 1½ oz dark rum
  • 1½ oz aged rum
  • 1 oz overproof rum
  • ¾ oz fresh lime juice
  • ½ oz falernum
  • ½ oz fresh grapefruit juice
  • ¼ oz pomegranate grenadine
  • 1 dash Angostura bitters
  • ½ barspoon absinthe

Steps

  1. Blend 1½ oz dark rum, 1½ oz aged rum, 1 oz overproof rum, ¾ oz fresh lime juice, ½ oz falernum, ½ oz fresh grapefruit juice, ¼ oz pomegranate grenadine, 1 dash Angostura bitters and ½ barspoon absinthe with a cup of ice until smooth, then pour into a hurricane glass.
  2. Garnish with a sprig of mint. Add a straw.
