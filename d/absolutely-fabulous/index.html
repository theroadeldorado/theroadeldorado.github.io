SHAKEN · FLUTE

Absolutely Fabulous

Vodka and cranberry shaken cold and topped with Champagne. Named for the TV comedy, and as fizzy and flippant as its heroines.

Ingredients

  • 1 oz vodka
  • 1½ oz cranberry juice
  • 3 oz Champagne

Steps

  1. Fill the shaker with ice.
  2. Add 1 oz vodka to the shaker.
  3. Add 1½ oz cranberry juice to the shaker.
  4. Cap the shaker and shake hard for about 12 seconds.
  5. Strain into a chilled flute.
  6. Top with 3 oz Champagne.
