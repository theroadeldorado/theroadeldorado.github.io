SHAKEN · HIGHBALL

San Francisco

Vodka and banana liqueur shaken with fresh orange juice and a dash of grenadine, then poured long over ice. Soft, fruity and a little retro.

Ingredients

  • 1 oz vodka
  • 1 oz crème de banane
  • 4 oz fresh orange juice
  • ¼ oz pomegranate grenadine

Steps

  1. Fill the shaker with ice. Add ice cubes to a highball glass.
  2. Add 1 oz vodka to the shaker.
  3. Add 1 oz crème de banane to the shaker.
  4. Add 4 oz fresh orange juice to the shaker.
  5. Add ¼ oz pomegranate grenadine to the shaker.
  6. Cap the shaker and shake hard for about 12 seconds.
  7. Strain into the highball glass.
  8. Garnish with an orange wheel on the rim.
