BUILT · HIGHBALL

Greyhound

Vodka and fresh grapefruit, with a sprig of rosemary.

Ingredients

  • 2 oz vodka
  • 4 oz fresh grapefruit juice

Steps

  1. Add ice cubes to a highball glass.
  2. Add 2 oz vodka to the highball glass.
  3. Add 4 oz fresh grapefruit juice to the highball glass.
  4. Give it a gentle stir.
  5. Garnish with a sprig of rosemary.
