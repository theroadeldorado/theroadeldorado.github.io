Caipirissima

A Caipirinha made with rum in place of cachaça: lime wedges muddled with sugar under crushed ice. Zesty, rustic and refreshing.

Ingredients

  • 1 lime, cut into wedges
  • ¾ oz simple syrup
  • 2 oz white rum

Steps

  1. Muddle 1 lime (cut into wedges) in a rocks glass.
  2. Add crushed ice to the rocks glass.
  3. Add ¾ oz simple syrup to the rocks glass.
  4. Add 2 oz white rum to the rocks glass.
  5. Give it a gentle stir.
  6. Garnish with a lime wheel on the rim.
