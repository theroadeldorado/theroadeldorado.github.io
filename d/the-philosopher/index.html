SHAKEN · COUPE

The Philosopher

Gin and melon liqueur shaken with lemon and a dash of orange bitters, then strained into a coupe and topped with prosecco. Light and bubbly, with ripe honeydew sweetness kept in check by the citrus.

Ingredients

  • 1 oz gin
  • ¾ oz melon liqueur
  • 1 dash orange bitters
  • ½ oz fresh lemon juice
  • 1½ oz Prosecco

Steps

  1. Fill the shaker with ice.
  2. Add 1 oz gin to the shaker.
  3. Add ¾ oz melon liqueur to the shaker.
  4. Add 1 dash orange bitters to the shaker.
  5. Add ½ oz fresh lemon juice to the shaker.
  6. Cap the shaker and shake hard for about 12 seconds.
  7. Strain into a chilled coupe.
  8. Top with 1½ oz Prosecco.
