Cherry Electric Lemonade

Four spirits and orange liqueur with fresh lemon, sugar and pomegranate grenadine, stretched with soda over ice. A fizzy, cherry-bright party lemonade with a serious kick.

Ingredients

  • ½ oz gin
  • ½ oz blanco tequila
  • ½ oz vodka
  • ½ oz white rum
  • ½ oz Cointreau
  • ¾ oz fresh lemon juice
  • ½ oz simple syrup
  • ½ oz pomegranate grenadine
  • Soda water, to top

Steps

  1. Add ice cubes to a highball glass.
  2. Add ½ oz gin to the highball glass.
  3. Add ½ oz blanco tequila to the highball glass.
  4. Add ½ oz vodka to the highball glass.
  5. Add ½ oz white rum to the highball glass.
  6. Add ½ oz Cointreau to the highball glass.
  7. Add ¾ oz fresh lemon juice to the highball glass.
  8. Add ½ oz simple syrup to the highball glass.
  9. Add ½ oz pomegranate grenadine to the highball glass.
  10. Top with soda water.
  11. Give it a gentle stir.
  12. Drop in a cocktail cherry. Add a straw.
